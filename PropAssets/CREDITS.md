# Credits and licences of the prop assets

Everything in the shipped bundle (`Assets/Mods/RealLifeOverhaul/Bundles/reallife_props`) is generated by the
scripts in `scripts/`. No third-party model or texture and nothing of Big Ambitions' own is inside it.

| Asset | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `props_base`, `props_mask` (the texture atlas) | painted by `scripts/atlas.py` | made for this mod; CC0 1.0 |
| all 77 meshes (`cone`, `barrier`, `stretcher`, `broom`, `guitar`, `newspaper_kiosk`, ...) | generated by `scripts/props.py`, `scripts/city_props.py` and `scripts/life_props.py` with `scripts/prop_kit.py` | made for this mod; CC0 1.0 |
| lettering in the atlas | drawn with Pillow's built-in default font, Aileron Regular (by Sora Sagano), thickened with a stroke | the font is CC0 1.0 |

## What the game contributes, and what it does not

- Two sizes (waist and head, `WAIST` and `HEAD` in `scripts/props.py`) and the suggested attachment offsets in
  `README.md` were measured on the game's character, read locally into the git-ignored `_reference/`. They are
  numbers; none of the game's vertices, triangles, UVs or textures are copied.
- At runtime the mod makes its materials from the game's `HDRP/Lit` shader. No shader is in the bundle.

## Depicted marks

"POLICE LINE DO NOT CROSS", "POLICE", "EMS", "PARAMEDIC" and "FIRST AID" are generic wording. The six-barred
emblem on the medic bag is drawn by `atlas.py` in the manner of the Star of Life, as the game's own ambulance
shows it; the first-aid box uses the ISO white cross on green, not a red cross.

"TAXI", "HOT DOGS", "NEWS", "HELP WANTED", "FRAGILE", "CAUTION WET FLOOR", "CITY MAP", "GRAND OPENING" and the
notices on the job board are generic wording. "THE DAILY POST" (the newspaper) and "QUICK BITE" (the delivery
bag, with a plate, fork and knife) are names made up here and are not meant to be anyone's mark; the magazine
covers, the map and the banknote are abstract drawings of no real publication, city or currency.

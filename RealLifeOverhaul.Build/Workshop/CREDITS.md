What Real Life Overhaul 0.1.0 contains, and where each part came from. The in-game Guide has the same in short ("Credits and licences").

| File | What it is | Where it came from |

## What of the game is used, and what is not

- No model, texture, animation, sound or other file of Big Ambitions is in the package.
- The clothes and shoes were shaped around measurements of the game's character (where the body, the foot and each hairstyle lie). The measurements are numbers; no vertex, triangle, UV or texture of the game's was copied.
- The animations were made on a skeleton built from the joint positions and bone names of the game's street character, read from the installed game (`CityAssets/scripts/game_rig.json`: numbers and names only), so that they play on the game's people. No animation of the game's is in the bundle, and no motion capture or third-party animation.
- While it runs, the mod uses what the game already has in memory: its shader for the mod's materials, its characters, vehicles and sounds. None of that is stored in the mod's files.
- The screenshots in `gallery/` and the thumbnail show the game, as any screenshot does.

## Third-party material that ships

- The lettering painted into the prop texture (for example "POLICE LINE DO NOT CROSS") was drawn with the font Aileron Regular by Sora Sagano, which is CC0 1.0 (public domain). It ships only as painted pixels.
- Nothing else. MakeHuman's CC0 hair was looked at and not used (`CharacterAssets/CREDITS.md`). No Mixamo or other library animation was used.

## Marks shown in the models

"POLICE", "EMS", "PARAMEDIC", "FIRST AID", "TAXI" and the like are generic wording. The emblem on the medic bag is drawn in the manner of the Star of Life, as the game's own ambulance shows it. "THE DAILY POST" and "QUICK BITE" are made-up names.

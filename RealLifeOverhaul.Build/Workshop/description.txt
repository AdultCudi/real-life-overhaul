[h1]Real Life Overhaul[/h1]
Real Life Overhaul adds everyday life to the city of Big Ambitions: things happen in the street, to other people and to you, and you can take part in them. Every part can be switched off by itself, and four presets set how much of it happens to you.

[h1]What it adds[/h1]

[b]Driving[/b]
[list]
[*]A camera behind your character on foot and behind the car when you drive.
[*]Each car gets the acceleration and top speed of its kind, an eased throttle and a speedometer.
[*]Car ownership: fuel, servicing, wear, breakdowns, insurance, registration and roadside help.
[*]A custom shop in any repair bay: paint, lamps, underglow, fitted parts and performance parts.
[*]Bicycles and motorbikes to hire or buy.
[*]People react to your horn: someone in your way who should not be there hurries clear, and someone with the light in their favour stops and glares.
[*]A fairer driving test: only what you could have helped is marked.
[/list]

[b]Accidents and emergency services[/b]
[list]
[*]Traffic accidents near you, with police, an ambulance, paramedics, a stretcher and a taped-off road.
[*]People hit by a car fall and can be hurt or killed. So can you, short of dying: the worst is a trip to hospital.
[*]Fires in cars, bins and shopfronts, with a fire engine and crew.
[*]A 911 contact on your phone for accidents, crimes and fires.
[*]Accidents come from drivers you can watch go wrong: speeders, red-light runners, late brakers and drunks at night. They may crash where it happens, run somebody down, drive off and be chased, or be pulled over.
[*]Traffic sees people in the road: cars brake for anyone in their way, sound the horn, wait and drive on.
[/list]

[b]Police and courts[/b]
[list]
[*]Street crime near you, and pickpockets, muggers, break-ins and scam calls against you.
[*]Police stops for red lights, speeding and reckless driving, pursuits, and officers on the beat.
[*]A police station in each district, arrest, a holding cell and a record that follows you.
[*]Courts: bail, a lawyer, a hearing, fines, community service, prison and civil claims.
[*]Driving licences: a driving school, a theory test, a driven test, penalty points and roadside checks.
[/list]

[b]City life[/b]
[list]
[*]People at work and about their day on the pavements, and a coffee cart you can buy from.
[*]Markets, parades, protests, fun runs, film shoots and fireworks on closed roads.
[*]Weather that changes grip, traffic, accidents and energy.
[*]A mayor, elections, city policies and building sites that progress over days.
[*]Chess, fishing, a night out, sights to see, clothes to buy, city sounds and a photo mode.
[/list]

[b]Home and family[/b]
[list]
[*]Hygiene, stress, a tidy flat, cooking, sleep, fitness, illness and a doctor.
[*]Small things that happen to you, more than 300 of them, without repeats until all have been seen.
[*]People to get to know, things to do together, and a morning paper about your city.
[*]A partner who moves in, an engagement, a wedding, children and relatives.
[*]A roommate: put up a notice or ask a friend. They pay a share of the rent every week and are at home when their hours say so.
[/list]

[b]Work and business[/b]
[list]
[*]Seven jobs you do yourself: flyers, courier, ride-hail, cleaner, shelf stocker, drinks cart and busker. Each is worked in the game's own panels, and most can be left to the game's clock for a little less pay.
[*]Careers with six employers: a rota, reviews, promotion and a CV.
[*]Pressure on the businesses you run: rivals, a protection racket, inspections, staff, suppliers and an audit.
[/list]

[b]Money and credit[/b]
[list]
[*]A credit rating from 300 to 850, worked out from how you pay your loans, what you owe, and your fines and court record. Banks lend more and charge less as it rises. Under 580 no bank lends.
[*]Finance, an app on your phone: your money, your rating and what moves it, your accounts and your cash flow, and loans without a trip to the bank.
[*]An overdraft, a credit card, a business loan against a shop's takings, car finance and a mortgage.
[*]Not paying has steps: a reminder, a late fee, a higher rate, collections, repossession and a claim in court. How far it goes follows the preset.
[*]A lender of last resort when the banks say no, and ways out of debt: a payment arrangement or bankruptcy.
[/list]

[b]The city's economy[/b]
[list]
[*]A central bank that sets a rate each month. Bank offers, overdrafts and cards follow it.
[*]Prices that rise or fall, with rents, wages, wholesale prices and takings following them.
[*]Booms, slowdowns and recessions that last months. A new save starts with three steady months.
[*]Districts with an income level: price above what a district will bear and customers stay away.
[*]Wholesale prices that move with the season, the weather and events. The paper announces a shortage or a glut a day ahead.
[/list]

[b]Investors and the market[/b]
[list]
[*]A tycoon may offer money for a share of a business that does well. Accept, refuse or counter.
[*]Pitch to angel investors, tycoons and venture firms. They ask questions and check your figures against your books.
[*]A stock market of the city's companies, moved by the day's news, with a commission and dividends.
[*]Ventures, an app on your phone for all of it, with what your buildings earn.
[/list]

[b]Running a business[/b]
[list]
[*]Staff with a going rate by job and district: raise requests, offers from rivals, strikes, overtime and sick pay.
[*]Insurance against fire, theft and an inspector's fine, with claims.
[*]Marketing campaigns with a cost, an end and a report of what they brought.
[*]A quarterly return, an accountant, and cash off the books with an audit risk that grows.
[*]Licences that cost money and run out.
[*]Company, an app on your phone for all of it.
[*]A staff rota planner: the customer forecast for each hour, the opening hours that pay for their staff, who to hire, and a rota at a press.
[*]A cleaning service: cleaners shared between your businesses on a round, and cleaning contracts from other businesses.
[*]Selling a business for what it is worth, to buyers who make offers with their own terms.
[/list]

[b]Growing a business[/b]
[list]
[*]Brands: shops of one type trade under one name, with recognition in each district and standards they share.
[*]Franchises: applicants open shops under your brand and pay an entry fee and a share of their takings.
[*]Online shops: orders from the whole city, picked from your real stock by your staff and sent by your vans or a courier.
[*]Property, an app on your phone: buy, let, repair and sell buildings, or run an estate agency.
[*]Own or lease one of the city's hotels, and put your own cars out on hire.
[/list]

[b]Getting around[/b]
[list]
[*]A minimap on screen while you are out in the city, and a GPS: a route along the roads to your destination, with the next turn and the distance left.
[*]Bus stops, bus routes and fares: click a stop and choose any stop in the city from a list. Where a street has room a bus pulls into a marked bay. The subway is a journey with a wait.
[*]A taxi called from your phone to where you are.
[*]Hotels to stay in, and holidays booked at the travel agencies.
[*]The mod's places on the city map, each with its own filter.
[*]The minimap shows the city's buildings and water under the roads.
[/list]

[b]The mod's own tools[/b]
[list]
[*]Planner, an app on your phone that lists what you have to do today and in the week ahead, with reminders and your own notes.
[*]Guide, an app on your phone with a page for every feature: what it is, how to start it, what it costs and which keys it uses.
[*]Four presets (Relaxed, Standard, Realistic, Hardcore), settings export and import, and every key rebindable.
[*]A tool that takes the mod out of a save, with a backup first.
[*]Every table in Finance, Ventures, Company and the Planner sorts by its headings, filters by kind and ends in a line of totals.
[/list]

[h1]How to start[/h1]
[list]
[*]Subscribe, start the game and load a save or begin a new one. A notice appears once for each new version; click it to open the Guide.
[*]The mod's page is in the game's Options, on the Mods tab. It starts with a search box, the Guide and the presets. The mod starts on Standard.
[*]The Guide and the Planner are apps on your phone. The phone shows eight apps a page: scroll the mouse wheel over them for the next page.
[/list]

[h1]Performance[/h1]
No frame-rate figures are claimed. Apart from the cameras, the mod's features do their work on timers (most of them once a second), not every frame. The mod also has a few optional switches for the base game's own drawing work; each says on its line what changes on screen, and none promises a gain.

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
[list]
[*]Built against the Steam version of Big Ambitions as of October 2026 (Steam build 25482473, Unity 2022.3.62f2).
[*]Windows. The mod's models and animations are built for Windows only.
[*]English only.
[*]One DLL, one text file and three asset files. No game files are replaced.
[*]While another camera mod such as "Immersion - RPG Camera" drives the camera, this mod's cameras stand aside and come back when it stops.
[*]It can be added to an existing save.
[/list]

[h1]Removing the mod[/h1]
[olist]
[*]In each save you want to keep, open the mod's options page, then Save tools, and press "Prepare this save for removing the mod". It backs the save up, removes what the mod put in it and saves.
[*]Quit to the main menu and unsubscribe, or switch the mod off under Mods.
[*]Restart the game.
[/olist]

[h1]Known limits[/h1]
The mod is new, and parts of it have had little play.
[list]
[*]Courts and prison, the family, hotels and two-wheelers have had little or no play from start to finish.
[*]The bus ride and the subway journey are a fade to black while time passes. There is no bus or train interior.
[*]A hotel is its door and lists of choices: you do not walk around a room. Most holidays are told as messages.
[*]On an outing that goes indoors your companion goes in at the door and the screen fades. Nobody sits at a table.
[*]A court hearing and prison are pages of text with choices.
[*]Children are never seen as characters, and a partner at home never lies down.
[*]One incident at a time: no crime starts while an accident, a fire or a street event is on.
[*]A job in progress ends when its save is loaded: you are paid what you were owed. A home routine or a leisure activity in progress is simply over.
[/list]
The Guide's "Known limits" page has the full list.

[h1]Reporting a problem[/h1]
[list]
[*]On the options page, under Diagnostics, switch on "Developer diagnostics" and make the problem happen again.
[*]Attach Player.log from that session. It is in %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Hovgaard Games\Big Ambitions and is overwritten when the game starts again.
[*]Add a screenshot, what you were doing, what you expected, the mod version (0.2.1) and which other mods are on.
[*]For stutter, or a frame rate that drops the longer you play: switch on "Developer diagnostics" before you start, play for ten minutes or more until it happens, and send Player.log from that session with your processor, graphics card and memory. The mod writes a line a minute there that shows where the time goes.
[/list]

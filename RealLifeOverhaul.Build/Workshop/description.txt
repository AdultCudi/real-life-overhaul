[h1]Real Life Overhaul[/h1]
Real Life Overhaul adds everyday life to the city of Big Ambitions. Things happen in the street, to others and to you. Work up a career, raise a family, stand for mayor, list your company or just ride the bus. Every part can be switched off by itself, and four presets set how much of it happens to you.

[h1]New in 1.0: the living economy[/h1]
The biggest update so far. What comes after it is tried first on the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3828304001]beta item[/url], for testers, on a separate save.
[list]
[*][b]A city of real people.[/b] Every home holds a household that works, pays rent and tax and shops at the businesses within reach. Your customers are those people. Births, deaths and moves change the city over the years, and developers build new homes when flats run short.
[*][b]Flare with real followers.[/b] Your followers are the city's residents. A good post brings real customers, every business has a rating and reviews from people who shopped there, and trends move what the city wants.
[*][b]Tech firms rebuilt.[/b] Game studios, software, AI labs, consultancies and data centres sell into markets sized from the city, against rivals who play by the same rules. You run them from your own desk.
[*][b]Supply and stock.[/b] Suppliers with contracts, delivery trips that drive the streets, and fresh food that spoils.
[*][b]Life events.[/b] Illness and hospital with a health plan, parties and company events, giving to charity, and a will with an heir to carry on as.
[*][b]Rivals you can meet.[/b] Rival owners are seen around the city, bid for your businesses by name and sell to each other.
[*][b]Schemes and inquiries.[/b] Dressed books, false claims and money to people with power, each with a real chance of being found out.
[*][b]Insurance, shifts and loyalty cards.[/b] An Insurance app for car, health, home and business cover, taxi and delivery shifts in your own car, and loyalty cards for your shops.
[*][b]A new interface.[/b] The HUD, BizMan, conversations, the interior designer and the phone's apps in the mod's own design, each with its own switch.
[*][b]Realistic graphics.[/b] Under Performance in the options, pick a Natural, Filmic or Vivid look and the whole picture follows the hour, the weather and the season. It is off until you choose a look, and at its Free cost it draws nothing extra.
[/list]

[h1]What else it adds[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]A living street.[/b] People chat, eat, queue and busk. Accidents, fires, crimes, street events and weather, with ambulances and police.
[*][b]Getting around.[/b] Buses, the subway, taxis, bicycles, a minimap with a GPS route, cars that handle by their kind, a garage and a driving school.
[*][b]Law and politics.[/b] Fines, arrests, courts and prison. A council and a mayor you vote for, or stand yourself.
[*][b]Home and family.[/b] Cooking, a partner, a wedding, children, friends and outings. Talk to anybody on the pavement.
[*][b]Education and careers.[/b] Courses, degrees and 25 professions with ranks up to the top seat, street jobs and gigs.
[*][b]Money.[/b] A credit rating, loans, a mortgage, a bank rate, booms and recessions, bills, and a stock market of some 136 companies you can list your own on.
[*][b]Business.[/b] Staff who ask for raises, brands and franchises, interiors that bring customers, a tech sector, property, your own bank, dealership and broadband company.
[*][b]A bigger city.[/b] Uptown, a new district, and a build mode to buy land and place buildings.
[*][b]Tools.[/b] A Planner and a Guide on your phone, presets, every key rebindable, permadeath if you want it, and a tool that takes the mod out of a save.
[/list]

[h1]How to start[/h1]
[list]
[*]Subscribe, start the game and load a save or begin a new one. A notice appears once for each new version; click it to open the Guide.
[*]The mod's options are in the game's Options, on the Mods tab, and on your phone in the Mod settings app. A search box finds any option. The mod starts on Standard.
[*]The Guide and the Planner are apps on your phone.
[/list]

[h1]Performance[/h1]
Apart from the cameras, buses in view and hair in motion, the mod does its work on timers, most of it once a second, not every frame.

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
[list]
[*]Made for the current build of Big Ambitions on Steam's public branch. On an older build the mod says so, and how to update the game. Windows only.
[*]The mod follows the game's language. Any other language, or a text not yet translated, is in English.
[*]One DLL, a text file for each language and six asset files. No game files are replaced. It can be added to an existing save.
[*]Made to work with Going Public, the multiplayer mod: what would put players out of step, such as fast forward, is set aside in a session.
[*]Works beside Empire Motors, Meaningful Interiors and Meaningful Ambience: where those already do something, this mod's part stays off. While a camera mod such as "Immersion - RPG Camera" drives the camera, this mod's cameras stand aside.
[/list]

[h1]Removing the mod[/h1]
In each save you want to keep, press "Prepare this save for removing the mod" under Save tools on the options page. Then unsubscribe and restart the game.

[h1]Known limits[/h1]
Some journeys, hotels, indoor outings, court and prison pass under a fade or as text, and children are never seen. The Guide's "Known limits" page has the full list.

[h1]Reporting a problem[/h1]
Switch on "Developer diagnostics" under Troubleshooting, make the problem happen again, and send Player.log from %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Hovgaard Games\Big Ambitions with a screenshot, the mod version (1.0.2) and your other mods.

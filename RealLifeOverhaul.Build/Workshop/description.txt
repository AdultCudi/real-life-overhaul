[h1]Real Life Overhaul[/h1]
Real Life Overhaul adds everyday life to the city of Big Ambitions: things happen in the street, to others and to you, and you can join in. Work your way up a career, stand for mayor, list your company, found a bank or a cloud, or just ride the bus. Every part can be switched off by itself, and four presets set how much of it happens to you.

[h1]What it adds[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]A living street.[/b] People chat, eat, queue and busk. Accidents with ambulances and police, fires, crimes, street events and weather. Police stations with officers at work.
[*][b]Getting around.[/b] City buses on many lines: walk on by either door, ride in real time or skip, and change between lines. The subway, taxis, bicycles, scooters, and a minimap with a GPS route.
[*][b]Driving.[/b] Cars that handle by their kind, chase and third person cameras, a garage for paint, parts and tuning, running costs and a driving school.
[*][b]Police, courts and the council.[/b] Fines, arrests, bail, hearings and prison. A council and a mayor you vote for, or stand yourself: a campaign, a debate, the count by district and the city's policies if you win.
[*][b]Street talk.[/b] Talk to anybody on the pavement: ask the way, swap numbers, invite them out or type your own words.
[*][b]Home and family.[/b] Cooking, chores, a partner, a wedding, children, friends, outings, leisure, a roommate and selfies.
[*][b]Education and careers.[/b] Courses at the school, and degrees and certificates at the university, with fees, loans and exams. 25 professions at the city's businesses and your rivals' companies, with ranks up to the top seat, freelance work and a firm of your own. Street jobs and gigs too.
[*][b]Standing.[/b] The city knows how rich, how known and how well thought of you are: reporters, a night show, promises remembered, invitations, lobbying, and bribes that can come out.
[*][b]Money and credit.[/b] A credit rating from 300 to 850, and a Finance app with loans, an overdraft, a card, car finance and a mortgage, with real consequences for not paying.
[*][b]The city's economy.[/b] A bank rate, prices that rise and fall, booms and recessions, districts with their own income and a district view on the map. The game's statements and taxes count what the mod charges and pays.
[*][b]Bills and the internet.[/b] Power and water from a utilities office, auto debit, rival internet providers, and a broadband company of your own.
[*][b]Markets and news.[/b] A stock market of some 136 companies with charts and orders, and your own company listed on it: results, dividends, a shareholders' meeting and takeover bids. The City Ledger and online papers. Flare, a social network with influencers.
[*][b]Running a business.[/b] Staff who ask for raises and may strike, insurance, marketing, a quarterly tax return, licences and a rota planner. Professional staff with skills, and a jobs board your rivals hire from too.
[*][b]Growing a business.[/b] Brands and franchises that keep their prices, online shops, investors, selling a business, your own bank and your own car dealership.
[*][b]Interiors and decor.[/b] Walls, floors and decor bring customers and let you charge a little more, each district expects its own standard, finishes wear, and an Interior tab in BizMan shows what to add next.
[*][b]The tech sector.[/b] Software and game studios, IT consultancies, security firms and AI labs, startups and venture capital, data centres, a cloud and in time a hyperscaler. Your data centres fill with server racks, and the city's tech companies have headquarters you can walk into.
[*][b]Property.[/b] Buy, let and sell buildings with a tenant in each unit, set your rental income, run an estate agency or a hotel, and hire out cars.
[*][b]Permadeath, if you want it.[/b] Off unless you switch it on: rare deaths after warnings, hunger among them, and an obituary in the City Ledger.
[*][b]Tools.[/b] A Planner and a Guide on your phone, presets, tables that sort and filter, settings export and import, every key rebindable, and a tool that takes the mod out of a save.
[/list]
The Guide in the game has a page for every feature: what it is, how to start it and what it costs.

[h1]How to start[/h1]
[list]
[*]Subscribe, start the game and load a save or begin a new one. A notice appears once for each new version; click it to open the Guide.
[*]The mod's options are in the game's Options, on the Mods tab, and on your phone in the Mod settings app. A search box finds any option. The mod starts on Standard.
[*]The Guide and the Planner are apps on your phone. Scroll the mouse wheel over the apps for the next page.
[/list]

[h1]Performance[/h1]
Apart from the cameras, buses in view and hair in motion, the mod does its work on timers, most of it once a second, not every frame.

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
[list]
[*]Made for the current build of Big Ambitions on Steam's public branch. On an older build the mod says so, and how to update the game. Windows only.
[*]The mod follows the game's language. Any other language, or a text not yet translated, is in English.
[*]One DLL, a text file for each language and three asset files. No game files are replaced. It can be added to an existing save.
[*]Made to work with Going Public, the multiplayer mod: what would put players out of step, such as fast forward, is set aside in a session.
[*]Works beside Empire Motors, Meaningful Interiors and Meaningful Ambience: where those already do something, this mod's part stays off. While a camera mod such as "Immersion - RPG Camera" drives the camera, this mod's cameras stand aside.
[/list]

[h1]Removing the mod[/h1]
In each save you want to keep, press "Prepare this save for removing the mod" under Save tools on the options page. Then unsubscribe and restart the game.

[h1]Known limits[/h1]
Some journeys, hotels, indoor outings, court and prison pass under a fade or as text, and children are never seen. The Guide's "Known limits" page has the full list.

[h1]Reporting a problem[/h1]
Switch on "Developer diagnostics" under Troubleshooting, make the problem happen again, and send Player.log from %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Hovgaard Games\Big Ambitions with a screenshot, the mod version (0.44.0) and your other mods.

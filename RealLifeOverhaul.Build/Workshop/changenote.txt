New
- Futures and options on the Stocks app's new Futures tab. The raw materials your factories buy now have prices that move at each market close, set by the city's exchange as the importers' prices. Buy a future to fix next week's price, with its goods delivered to your warehouse or factory or settled in cash, or buy calls and puts on materials and on the city's larger companies. Managers for your empire: on the Company app's new Managers tab, appoint a manager for any business, warehouse or factory from its own staff, and a regional manager for a district from your headquarters. They answer raises and rivals' offers, agree overtime, put in claims, set the week's orders and prices, write the rota and replace staff from the job board, inside the limits you set, with mistakes in line with their skill, a monthly report, and every decision can be undone. Ready-made designs for the mod's own businesses: the interior installation firms now offer designs for a Car Showroom, the tech firms and a Training Academy, as they do for the game's businesses, at every building size the game has designs for. They are made from the game's own designs and furniture at the game's own prices, and a showroom's keep the middle of the floor clear for its cars. It has its own switch on the options page.

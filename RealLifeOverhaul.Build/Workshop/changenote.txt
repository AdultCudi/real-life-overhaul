0.4.9

New
- A car dealership of your own. Found a Car Dealership in a shop unit with BizMan, hire salespeople and run it from the Dealership app. Buy cars new from the wholesaler, used from their owners after an inspection and a haggle, or at the trade auction against other dealers. Customers come by the hour by the building's promotion, your staff close what reaches your floor and take them on test drives, and you answer the rest. Sell on your own finance and with warranties; the takings and the costs are in the game's statement and tax.

Fixed
- With Empire Motors switched on, the chase camera works again. V in a car is this mod's camera key once more; Empire Motors' view from the driver's seat, which on most cars only switched to the game's camera for driving inside a building and let the chase camera go, is kept off while the key is V. To use that view, give this mod's camera key another key in the options.

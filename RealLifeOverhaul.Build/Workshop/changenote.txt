New
- Your bank's page now shows its profit a day, this month so far and last month, beside the month's own figures.

Fixed
- Your cash could go under nothing while your overdraft stayed unused, because it only covered the bills due at midnight, worked out beforehand. It now pays as soon as anything leaves your cash under nothing, up to its limit, and looks again every game hour.
- Bus stops could show as a blank white shape when the mod's props had not loaded, or loaded after the stop was built. A stop is now made again with its model once the props are in, plain coloured shapes stand in when a prop cannot be drawn, a stop can always be clicked, and Player.log and a notice name the cause.
- The customers a shop's interior brings no longer vanish for the rest of the day when you stock a shelf, move an item or change marketing, and the Interior tab's customers page shows each part as it is.
- A job's review and clocking-on texts sent you to the office at the address of a shop, and the shop's door only leads into the shop. They now send you to the help-wanted board on the pavement outside it, where your boss waits, and name the shop it stands outside. The Career app, the map marker and the Guide say so too, and if an employer's board is gone the review is held by phone.
- Sales, support and customer service staff at a tech firm had no desk to sit at, because the game's desks did not take their skill; every desk does now, and the Staff and roles app, the hiring pages and the Team tab say which workstation a role needs and why someone has no seat.
- The notice that new buildings north of Manhattan were not whole buildings came back at every load, because homes and warehouses let to tenants were taken for unfinished ones. They count as whole now, and the notice only shows when more buildings than last time are not whole.

0.7.3

Fixed
- The mod's special buildings carry their own names wherever the game names a building. The utilities office is City Utilities, and the courthouse, the police stations and the hotels have theirs: on the building's card in the street and on the city map, on the destination marker and in the GPS, at the top of the screen inside, and in the city map's search, with the address beside it as before. Your saves are not changed. The option 'Special buildings carry their own names' switches it off. Hudson City University is now in a building of its own, never the City Utilities office's: a save that was enrolled there follows it to the new address. It is on the city map with Manhattan Business School and the hospitals' wards in a new row, 'Places to study', with its card, 'set destination' and the map's search. At Manhattan Business School a class of yours is joined the game's way: go in, press Study at one of its desks, and the game's panel has a button 'Attend' with the course's name (or 'Sit the exam' when it is due) beside the game's own courses, which are as they were. At the university and on the wards the game's panel over the place offers the same on your interact key. When a class begins, a notice says how to join it, or why you cannot yet, where it is held and how long it runs. The Campus app's buttons still work.

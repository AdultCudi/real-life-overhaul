New
- In a Going Public session the city is one city for every player, the host's: its economy (the bank rate, prices, the good and bad times, the districts, supply), its government (the mayor, the council, policies, elections, petitions and the building sites) and its stock market, with the same prices on every screen. A guest's vote, donation, petition, complaint or trade counts in the host's city, a guest who leaves carries on from the city as it was, and each player's money, shares and businesses stay their own.

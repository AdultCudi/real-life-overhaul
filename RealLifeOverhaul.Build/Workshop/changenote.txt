0.19.1

New
- Developer diagnostics: a stutter's line in Player.log now counts every part of the mod, its work done step by step and what it does when the game announces something (a new hour, a door), so it always names the part of the mod that took the time.

Fixed
- A short pause the first time an ambulance, a patrol car or a fire engine was sent out in a city, while the sound of its siren was being made; the sounds are now made while the loading screen is still up.
- Short hitches when the game was paused, while the mod looked through the whole scene for characters with detailed hair or finished work left over from the loading screen; both now wait for the black screen at a door or a loading screen.
- A hitch the first time one of the mod's own panels opened, while it looked for the game's font.
- Short hitches near police stations and at accidents, arrests, road closures and street events, when the mod looked for the nearest lane of a road by going through every one of the city's 19,000 lane points, sometimes dozens of times in one frame; it now looks only near the place, and finds exactly the same lane.
- Hitches in the first minute after a save loaded. The roads for the GPS and the buses, the minimap's pictures, the bus routes and the performance options are now prepared while the loading screen is still up, the city's road data is looked for once instead of twice, and the phone's contact pictures are drawn a little at a time.
- A stutter at the stock market's close at 16:00 and its open at 09:30; both are now worked out over a few frames, with exactly the same prices, and after a sleep or a skip the market catches up smoothly.
- Heavier frames at midnight and when saving: the tech sector's people, the city's job vacancies, Utilities' accounts and Flare's shops are now looked up directly instead of one by one, the stock market and the tech sector write their save data without piles of throwaway text, and the workers on your job shifts change their looks only when it causes no stutter.
- A heavy frame at the start of each game day, and a smaller one each hour, with many businesses: several parts of the mod each had the game draw a shop's customers again, three or four times over in one frame. Now each shop is drawn again once, only where its footfall really changed, a little each frame within the hour, and the economy reads the day's books once.

New
- On a day worked from home, your computer has a Start the working day button beside Pass Time, and Work late once the day is worked: you sit down at it and work, with fast forward and skip, as for an online class.

Fixed
- The game's panel no longer closes when you press a class's button at a desk of the school or the university, or Start working at your desk at work: you are taken there. At the university the class's button is now on the same key as the game's own Start.
- At a desk computer at work you now sit on its chair and type, rather than stand beside it.
- A working day at home no longer leaves you unable to move when it ends: you stand up from the computer and can walk away.

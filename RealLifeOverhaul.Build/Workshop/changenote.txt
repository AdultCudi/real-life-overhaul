New
- Uptown: Manhattan carries on north past 1st Avenue into a new district of three rows of blocks from Willow to Lake Street, with pavements, lamps, traffic lights, parking, people and traffic. It can be switched off under Uptown in the options.
- Uptown is built up from the first day, with businesses, homes and offices to rent or buy in BizMan, and a third of its units free to rent.
- Build mode from the Construction app: buy land square by square, zone it and place one of the city's designs, with demand bars for each use and the city's developers building where a use is wanted.
- Corner buildings in build mode, seven of them, with live shop signs: the business's name, For lease, and the address number.
- Your own construction company in the Construction app, or one of the city's eight builders hired to build for you: plans, planning permission, three inspections and the handover.
- Construction sites that rebuild whole buildings, wrapped to the roof in the game's own scaffolding.
- The building trades at a works site, from labourer to the city's building inspector, an architects' practice, new courses and three new employers.
- The city around construction: the building code set by the elected government, rigged tenders, unpaid bills in court and the City Ledger's building of the year.
- Show flats for blocks sold off plan, and refits of any unit you rent or own.
- Redevelopment in Uptown: knock down, build anew, join, split, extend and convert, by you and by the city's developers.
- Uptown's landmarks: Summit Tower, Uptown Galleria and Crest Field, put out to tender, with Uptown FC at home every Saturday.
- Five of the rival builders on the stock market, building materials on the raw materials exchange and a project's hedge.
- Uptown's own special places: a Vantander Bank branch, agencies, stores, a supermarket, a clinic, a car dealership, an importer, a wholesale store and a petrol station.
- Uptown's offices are employers in the Career app, and its builders have someone at the front desk to quote and be hired.
- Two new bus lines from the subway at 9th and 2nd Street, and benches, bins and trees on Uptown's pavements.
- Two parks in Uptown with lawns, paths, a fountain, a statue and benches.
- Birchwood School on Uptown's west square, with its own hand-made buildings: buy it and run it, and enrol your children from the Campus app's Schools tab.
- Show on map in the Construction app, and the construction game's next steps in the game's objectives.
- Contact groups in the Contacts app, with search across every group.

0.39.1

Fixed
- Resting or sleeping in a hotel room no longer gives you a cold when it rains. The room counted as standing in the street, so an hour of rain while you were up there made you ill.
- A night or a nap in a hotel room now counts as sleep. It used to count as hours awake, so sleep debt and stress went up overnight instead of down.
- You can now go up to your hotel room while the game is paused. It used to say you were busy with something else.
- With Developer diagnostics on, the log's line for a long frame now names the mod's costliest piece of work in that frame, and also the newest when they differ. It used to name only the newest, which could blame a small job for another's cost.

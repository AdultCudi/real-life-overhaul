0.2.2

New
- Traffic sees people in the road: cars brake, sound the horn and wait. People react to your horn too.
- Accidents come from drivers you can watch go wrong, instead of appearing at a distance.
- A rota planner in the Company app: a customer forecast, and a ladder of plans from the week as it is to the last person worth hiring.
- The rota gives every employee the weekly hours they ask for, and puts shifts on the hours customers come.
- The more skilled get their hours first. Staff the business has no hours for are named, so you can let them go.
- New phone apps: Services (a shared cleaning crew, and selling a business), Brands (brands and franchises), Web shop (online shops), Property (buildings, an estate agency, hotels, car hire).
- A roommate who pays a share of the rent and lives in your flat.
- The minimap shows buildings and water.
- The options page is in ten groups with short names; each option explains itself when you point at it, and test tools are tucked away under Troubleshooting.
- Tables in Finance, Ventures, Company and the Planner sort, filter and total.
- Panels no longer cover a building's info box. Fishing, dancing and chess use the game's own bar.
- The mod's cameras stand aside for other camera mods, and its route keeps to the minimap beside Voogle Route.

Fixed
- The game froze for seconds when an ambulance crew stepped out.
- Short freezes the first time you drove, at the start of a driving lesson, and at a petrol station.
- A car in the custom shop shot backwards when the shop closed.
- The driving test marked things you could not help. Where it ends is now a box painted on the road.
- A traffic car's horn sounded as if it were beside you when it was far away.

1.0.19

New
- Mom can now be told to stop getting in touch by herself, and with her option on she contacts you at most once a day. Skills can be set not to fade without practice. A driving licence now lasts two of your save's years, never fewer than 120 days. The Team page of Open Roles gives everyone shown a raise in one go, with the monthly cost first, and can match rivals' offers by itself up to the limit of the automatic raises. Data centres can now have a leased line, 5 to 100 Gbps, bought on the Internet page of the Utilities app in every district, which lifts the connection limit on racks. An AI model can now be made for people, small businesses or large companies, and changed later from its page for a cost in work, days and customers. Selling a tech firm now has terms. Take part of the price in the buyer's shares, keep a slice of the firm, take an earn-out, or stay as the boss on a contract of three or four years. A talk with a person now says in its header how old they are: the exact age for somebody you know or have asked, and the ten years they look to be for a stranger, in street talk, calls, meetings, your partner's talks and your flatmate's. A private driver button stands on the bar, in the RLO interface and on the game's own top bar, while you have a driver, with its own key (Insert, changeable in Settings) and the driver's state on it. You can play your own music and sounds in gigs: switch on Play your own music in gigs in the options and press Your gig music folder, which makes folders for busking, the drinks cart's calls and till, and a taxi's car door. It is off by default, only you hear it, and the game's own sound plays if there are no files. The game's rivals now own buildings by their place in the Rivals app: the top rivals own the most, the last a handful, and a rival's page shows its buildings, their rent and its fortune beside the weekly income the game ranks by. You can now run your estate agency as a business of its own: owners of flats ask agencies to pitch for their blocks and pay a share of the rent, rival agencies compete for the same blocks, and your own buildings can be put under your agency. Each rival now has a head office, a real office building marked on the map, where they stand in office hours and talk business. Single flats and studios of the city's residential buildings now come up for sale in the Property app. Buy one as your home on cash or a mortgage, with a monthly service charge and property tax, then let it, sell it, or buy the whole building later. The police are a career now: pass the academy, go on patrol from a station's front desk, answer the incidents the city raises near you with the danger shown first, see each arrest through to its hearing, and rise from cadet to lieutenant.

Fixed
- A tech firm's sale accepted at the desk inside the firm now completes when you step outside, or can be called off on the Exits page. Other mods that look up Uptown's buildings by address while they start find them. Switching off Staff pay, raises, strikes and sick pay now also stops a rival's offer to your staff, strike threats and sick days, and their notices name the options. The applicants' button on Open Roles says Offer, because they answer the next day. The Rivals app's rival head no longer cuts the buildings and their worth, the league shows its week column only once a week has passed, the Company overview's waiting rows no longer join two sentences with a comma, and the phone's Up next lists a street event once.

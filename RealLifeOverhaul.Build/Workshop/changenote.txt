- Buses: you are seen getting on and off. Board and you walk to the front door, pay, step in, tap your card on the validator and sit down by the middle door, or stand and hold a strap when the bus is full. You ride as it pulls away, and from above its roof is taken off so that you can see in. At your stop the doors open, you get up and step off by the middle door, and the bus leaves. A full bus now takes you, standing.

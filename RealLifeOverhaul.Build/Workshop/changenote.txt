- An answer you type on a call is read the right way round: good news no longer gets a sorry, and an unclear answer gets a neutral reply.
- A difficult moment at home now comes from what is really going on, such as long nights at work, money trouble or not calling, instead of at random.
- A call, text or outing with your partner in Social now counts as talking, so they no longer say you have not talked in days.
- A bicycle or motorbike parked when you saved could not turn after the save was loaded: its front wheel steered, but it went straight on. It now turns as it should when you get back on.
- Waking up no longer brings a pile of notifications at once. The morning summary comes only when you wake from a sleep, with the hours you really slept, and says good morning only in the morning; the city's news from while you slept (the weather, city policies, works next door, what is on, your papers) comes with it in one notification, or two. The city's requests, protests, contract offers and a better credit rating each have a switch for their notifications.

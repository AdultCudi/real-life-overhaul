Fixed
- A short stutter the first time some phone and computer panels opened while playing. The game's font is now found again by its name, never by a search of the whole scene.
- The left pane of the apps, the white card beside the tables, drew section heads over the text above them and wrapped lines over the next row, because the card had a fixed height and Unity's layout squeezed every text to fit. The card is now rebuilt and scrolls: the title, its line and its status chips stay at the top, the buttons and the Guide link at the foot, and what lies between scrolls with the mouse wheel. Each section is a card with its own heading, each fact is a label and a value on one line (a long value wraps under itself), and a career ladder is a list of rungs with the pay beside each name, what it asks under it and your own rung marked, whatever the language or the length of the text.
- Sitting at a recruitment office's desk without the Basic HR course gave you nothing. The desk now always offers two choices: register with the jobs board, which needs no course, or recruitment, which shows the Basic HR page if that course is not complete.
- A thing bought on the Marketplace now has a clear way to be collected. The route ends on the pickup point at the seller's door, a parcel stands there, and the door's panel offers Collect, also after a load. If you come by car, a notice tells you to get out. The seller holds it for three days, the Yours tab says until when, and after that the money returns to you.
- Two people in your company could have the same name. The game checks a new name only against the staff it knew when it first looked, so seekers, candidates waiting and your tech payroll were missed. Every new name in the tech sector is now checked against all names in use, and people who already share a name are given a new one, with a notice. A hire the game turns down no longer leaves a candidate waiting in the staff app, and an applicant who cannot start, because the business is gone or the game will not take them on, is now reported to you.
- A limit on products in tech firms was too low and not explained. Your firms could have only 16 products being built or on sale between them, and withdrawn products piled up in the list. Each firm can now have 12 and all your firms 36, the 12 newest withdrawn products are kept, and the Tech app says how many you have and why another cannot be started. A product holds 24 features at most, and its page and the Guide now say so.
- Alex and Mom now have one fixed look for every player, in a new game and in an old save, and Alex, a woman, is the same at home as your roommate, at gatherings and on the phone. A roommate who is one of your friends keeps that friend's look, and your partner is drawn at the same age at home as when you go out. A save that held two looks for one person is put right on load, with a notice.

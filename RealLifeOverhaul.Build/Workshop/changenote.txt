New
- The phone feels like a real phone. The mod's apps now stand in five folders on the home screen: Money, Work and study, Social and life, Business and Settings. Each folder's tile shows small pictures of its apps and their numbers added up; click it to open the folder, and Back or Esc to close it. A dock along the foot of the phone keeps two favourites on every page, the Planner and Flare to begin with. Right click an app to move it into another folder, onto the home screen or into the dock; your layout is kept for every save. The three bank apps are one Banks app now, with the bank picked at the top of its page. 'App folders on the phone' on the options page switches it off and brings back the plain pages. The Mod settings app looks like the game's help (F1) now, in dark colours: the groups and sections in a list on the left, and on the right each option with a short line about it and a switch, a slider, a list or a button. Search and the presets work as before, and a key changes with one press.

0.2.0: Money and credit: you have a credit rating from 300 to 850, worked out from how you pay. Under 580 no bank lends. A Finance app on the phone shows your money, your rating and your cash flow, and lets you borrow without going to the bank. There is an overdraft, a credit card, a business loan, car finance and a mortgage, real consequences for not paying, a lender of last resort, and ways out of debt. Getting around: call City Cabs for a taxi to where you are. Every bus stop now stands all the time, and where a street has room a bus pulls into a marked bay so traffic passes it. The bus countdown shows how long the bus will really take. Work: gigs pay about 2.8 times what they did and no two shifts are alike. Cleaning uses the game's own mop and dirt, to 100%. A shift in progress is paid out when a save is loaded. Life: a court's custody is served in the station's cell. Your partner sits down at home, and you can sit on a bench on a walk. More goes on in the street. Family, courts, friends, leisure, the council and the dispatch desk now use the game's own interface. Fixed: hotel reception closed as soon as it opened.

Also new since 0.1.3. The rest of the mod's own boxes are replaced by the game's interface. Jobs: every gig and shift has the game's job panel and the game's panel over the place you work at, and most can be left to the game's clock at 85% of the pay. At the drinks cart you take your place by walking behind it, wear a uniform and can call out to passers-by, and carts are solid. The cleaner's spills are all on the floor at once. Shift times show a start and an end. Home: hygiene, stress and home are bars under the game's own, and cooking is a list opened from a cooker or from the fridge's panel. Garage: a repair bay's own panel says what is due and has a Garage button for the service, the custom shop and your vehicles. The Garage key is gone. Hotels, the police station's desk and cell, and the driving school with its theory test use the game's panel and list. The Guide is an app on the phone, and the welcome is a notice. On the map, an unticked row hides its places in the street too. Minimap and GPS: a small map of the streets around you is on screen whenever you are out in the city, with a route along the roads to your destination, the next turn, the distance left and the time you should arrive. The marker goes when you arrive. Two quick clicks on the city map set a destination. Buses and the subway now use the game's own interface: point at a bus stop, its shelter or the standing bus and click. The stop shows the line and the next bus, and "Choose a stop" opens a list of every bus stop in the city with the time and the fare. Pick a stop while you wait and you board by yourself when the bus pulls in. A line's last stop can be boarded from. Buses: nobody waits more than about 20 minutes of the game clock for a bus, and a bus waits at the stop for you instead of leaving after a few seconds. Bus stops are there when you arrive: after loading a save, a taxi ride or the subway, a stop near you is put up at once, and when driving they are put up from further away. The driving school takes its car back: when a lesson or test ends you are asked to step out, and the car can no longer be kept and driven. An officer posted at a junction stands watch and no longer waves at traffic that is following the lights. Bicycles are faster: about 38 km/h for the city bicycle and 50 for the road bicycle.

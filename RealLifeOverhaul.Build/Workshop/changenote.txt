1.0.4

Fixed
- On the RLO interface's checkout no goods were listed, so nothing could be chosen at a counter (coffee shops, fast food, restaurants) and taxes could not be paid at the tax office. The goods and the tax rows show again, and an order of nothing is refused in words instead of waiting in a queue.
- Food in a bag in your hands could not be eaten on the RLO interface's item panel. Eat, Drink, Equip, Read and Place for each thing you carry are buttons of the panel again.
- When you ordered furniture or supplies by phone, the plus on a row of the items list only played the error sound. It adds the item to the order again.
- On the RLO interface a box could not be taken off a hand truck or out of a vehicle, and the fuel gauge and the vehicle's condition were missing while driving. A hand truck, a cart and a scooter show their cargo tiles again, Manage cargo has Take for the chosen stack, and the card you see while driving shows the fuel with its range, the condition and how much the car carries.
- With the RLO interface on, most Korean and Chinese text was not drawn at all. Their letters come from the game's own fonts, which stand taller than the interface's, and a line too tall for its box was dropped. The game's own Japanese, traditional Chinese and Greek words, and names typed in those scripts, were lost the same way. Every line is drawn now, in the game's fonts at the interface's own line height. The mod's older screens, shown while the RLO interface is off, could drop a line in those languages the same way where its box was short, and keep it now.
- On the RLO interface, EconoView's profit chart drew its bars over the whole window when a business lost about the same amount every day. A bar chart now always starts at zero, with losses hanging down from the zero line, and no chart draws outside its own box.
- With the RLO interface on, an app another mod puts in the full menu's top bar, as Empire Motors and Empire Bank do, could not be found anywhere. These apps are now on the phone's home screen and in All apps under Other mods, and the first two stand on the app row at the bottom, each with its own picture. A button another mod adds to the building's panel or to a BizMan page is offered there too.
- In BizMan on the RLO interface, a button that cannot do what it says now tells you why at the foot of the page instead of only playing the error sound, the open switch also works on a business's Building and Real estate sections, the page that starts a business says that fitting it out comes afterwards, a building one of the mod's features holds says whose it is instead of For rent, and a business that power, water or the internet closes again after you open it tells you why every time.
- On a Mac, a bug report left out the game's log, the Guide named the wrong folder for it, and RLO Texts tried to run its packs, which only Windows can. A key for Your own AI is now kept there until you close the game.
- On the RLO interface, a day's band in Finance's Cash flow only played the error sound when pressed, and so did the Marketplace's sort choice. The band now folds that day's lines away and brings them back, and the sort choice puts the listings by price or newest first.

New
- RLO Texts: with the RLO Texts or RLO Texts Lite pack from the Workshop, a small model on your own PC writes the words of Flare replies and posts, messages from people, the city's answers, the rival tycoons and the City Ledger's briefs, offline. Without it, or when it is slow, the written lines stay. People answer what you write to them on Flare, in a message, a post or a reply, and the author of a post may answer your reply under it. The posts of the city's people, its accounts, its businesses and its influencers have comments under them, written by RLO Texts for each kind of post, and the thread says how many more replied. On a graphics card the model runs at normal priority, so it keeps up while you play; on the processor it stays at low priority, so the game comes first. Invite somebody out in a Flare message, for a coffee, a meal, drinks, the cinema, a walk or a workout, and the game decides yes, maybe or no. A yes names a place and a time and books the outing as a call would, and somebody you had not met joins People as met online, with the face they have on Flare. Flare: every account has a picture beside its name (faces drawn by the game's own portrait camera, the game's logos for businesses, emblems for the city's offices), and some of the city's posts carry a photo of the place, taken where you are. Both are options. While a save loads, Flare draws the faces you are likely to see first behind the loading screen, for two seconds at most, and keeps every face it has read for the session. Street talk: walk up to anybody on the pavement and talk to them in the game's own dialogue panel. Everybody has a name, a job, a mood and a memory of the day, and some have no time. Ask the way and the game sets your destination, swap numbers and they join People with the look you saw, invite them for a coffee and the game decides, and tell them about your business. With RLO Texts the model words the whole conversation, while the game decides how it goes and names the places.

Fixed
- You can like the comments under other people's posts on Flare. Street talk: the blue button now follows the moment and what they just said: how their day is going, the weather or the hour, the street event, what they do and whether they like it, thanks after the way somewhere, their name and number after a compliment that landed, and two people no longer open the same way. Say something of your own is always one press away, and Esc says goodbye. People who stop give you at least four turns and more when the talk goes well, not having washed cools them a little instead of freezing everyone, and nobody says the same written line to you twice in a day.

New
- Permadeath, an ending you can choose: off unless you switch it on, and kept with each save. With it on, your character can die for good, but only after a warning: a crash at very high speed, being hit by fast traffic on foot, a mugging refused, the private lender's men or a flu left untreated past three warnings. The game is saved at once, the City Ledger prints a front page in memory with their life's story, and the save can be opened again to read it but never played. It is off in a Going Public session and during the game's tutorial and missions.

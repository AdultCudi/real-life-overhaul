New
- Nothing yet. Food spoilage has a speed slider in the options, from a quarter of the time to four times as long.

Fixed
- The Web shop can open a site from the RLO interface and change its delivery way, promise and quality, and the old Web shop app now sets the delivery fee, packing, night orders, areas, titles, refunds and couriers and shows the shop as a customer sees it. The RLO Campus can attend a class, do homework and sit an exam, Career has Start the working day, Work late, the interview place, Register, the employers' addresses with Show on map and the whole Management list with Move and the executive seat, Open Roles can move a person to another role and brief a crew for the right data centre, Persona shows what each goal tier asks, and the money apps follow cash as it moves.
- A notice or button that opens Career, Campus, Open Roles, Your bank, Utilities, Marketplace, Stocks, the Planner, the economy app, Flare or a photo you have just taken now lands on the right page of the new interface. A screen that fails is asked again in the next city and you are told once. Turning the interface off in play no longer leaves a card or Build mode half drawn, and a mod that fails no longer takes the whole interface off.
- Starting a business in BizMan now shows what each type is from the game's guide, its fees, the building's photo, size and lease, and Enter in the name box starts it. Requirements have their help links, long lists of contracts and campaigns scroll, and the prices, capacity and settings pages carry what the game's own screen says.

- The Stocks desk now has the sector table with its 5, 21, 63 and 100 day moves, the news, the rate line and the index page, the company figures and results, more columns, bid and ask, status and impact on the order, and real cost and gain in the portfolio. The Company tax tab shows back tax owed, a settled case and the estate tax, and a Tech firm's page shows its crew, connection and risks.
- A fridge, wardrobe or street cart shows every button, paged when there are many. The car's steering assist can be switched from the vehicle panel. The surgeon is the game's own screen again, with its face sliders, colours and zoom. A person's card lists every skill and every demand, hover cards keep their security line and long requirement lists say how many more there are, and manage cargo lists what is inside a box.
- The map's quick chips and layers work on lines in a folded group, a building under the pointer shows its facts, a station previews as you point at it and the taxi's button works on the place card. The Property and Services tables sort by every column, filter and total as the old tables did, the tenant, listing and district pages say more, and long private driver and prompt lists no longer lose buttons.
- The clock follows the game's 12 hour option on the bar, the pause menu and the notice list. The day result under the money shows what the game shows. Money and clock hover cards are back. Help and Report bug are back in the full menu. Season on the bar can be switched off. Error notices have their own look.
- My Employees filters combine again, its table has the age and hours columns, a person's page shows the days worked, the sick line and the HR manager button, candidate rows can discard, negotiate and hire, a single text in Contacts can be deleted, two people of one name keep their own faces, a study session shows its diploma before Start, and long question boxes show whole.
- The design window in Build mode has its Fits here, Corner buildings and City designs filters back and its tabs count the designs they show, the Workshop upload terms press the game's own buttons, a saved sign colour can be removed from the sign editor, the designer shows another mod's tools and panels and says when a business needs no cameras, and long tool panels scroll.
- A motorbike stand could stand in the lane to a petrol station's repair bays, where a truck crashed into it. Stands, bicycle racks and bike docks now keep out of every lane, bay, pump and parking spot.
- The Stocks tables sort by every column and the Futures tab shows held and offered futures in one table with the week, held, worth and gain, Bots show each firm's deepest fall and trades, BizMan shows the sales at each price on a goods row and each warehouse's stock, slots, drivers and inventory buttons, the interior designer's search shows the game's suggestion and takes Tab, its catalogue shows more rows in a tall window, the Economy goods chart keeps a steady scale on a young save, and times follow the 12 hour clock option.
- Two people of one name no longer share a face in Contacts, a rival's staff or a company question. A candidate's business can be picked beside the table. Demands carry the game's own explanation, a person's page shows their gender, a sick employee's row says so, the activity slider shows its keys and a health plan offer names the plan and its HR manager.
- On the RLO interface every list and panel that scrolls can also be dragged with the mouse as the game's own can, and a vehicle purchase lists every warehouse, a long checkout scrolls under its total, the market filter takes several types, a box of mixed goods shows its contents over its tile, the season size option and the clock and money cards work in the bars, the player's portrait stands at the needs, no control hint or objective is cut, the phone gives way to another mod's screen only while it is open, other mods' apps have a tile on the row, and a screen that stepped aside after an error says so in Mod settings.

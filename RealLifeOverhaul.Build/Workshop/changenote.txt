1.0.12

Fixed
- A phone you put away can always be brought back in the new interface, by a phone button in the bar or the Phone key, also when another mod pages the phone's apps. Chinese, Korean and other non Latin text is no longer left blank when a screen first opens.
- Every drop-down list in the new interface now scrolls with the mouse wheel, by dragging its scroll bar or the list, and with the keys or the pad, opens with the chosen option in view, and shows a long option whole.
- The Campus diary and Coming up no longer list classes after a course's last one, a course's place line no longer repeats its room, and the How a class works note reads one sentence to a line.
- A traffic light that stays on yellow for longer than a junction's cycle no longer counts against a person crossing, so the police do not warn or fine them for it. The mod also no longer takes over a car that is in the middle of a junction, which could have kept that light yellow.
- Leaving a city no longer logs errors for shops in Uptown and Cedar Heights while their customers were being worked out.

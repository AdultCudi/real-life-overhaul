0.51.0

New
- Companion tools can read prices, bills and business planning data, and ask which changes the mod allows before making them. The Guide and the Branches tab of Your bank now say why a bank branch looks like a loss every day in the game's statement: only its costs are booked there, while its fees and the business it brings are your bank's income at your headquarters. Lose touch: on a person's page in People, More about them now offers Lose touch. After the game's own question they leave your phone, and somebody new can take their place. Family, your old friend, a partner and anyone you share a home, plans or money with stay, and the page says why. Once your life is full, asking for a number says so instead of maybe next time, and an acquaintance you have not spoken to for 60 days may drift away to make room. A Marketplace app on the phone: buy used goods and cars from the city's people, collect them at their door or have them brought home, fix them up and clean them, and sell them on to buyers who haggle. Clients also ask for freelance visits there, from detailing a car to a move with a van, done at their door on the game's work panel or left to a helper, with a rating from each client. Being a landlord: your tenants now have a satisfaction, report repairs you can send a contractor to or do yourself at the building's door, complain and leave when unhappy, and the city inspects the buildings you let. Send a reminder or offer a payment plan for rent owed, hire a lettings agency or a property manager, and see each building's rent roll and what a sale would leave. Seasons: what your customers buy follows the season of the Calendar, with more coffee, tea, alcohol, gifts and flowers in winter and more ice cream and cold drinks in summer, and on some winter days it snows, forecast the evening before, the ground turns white and traffic and people in the street slow down. People you meet can hold a position: a recruiter, an importer's buyer, a wholesaler's manager, a law firm's partner, an investor, a city official or a landlord. Introductions by family and friends bring them more often, and as you grow closer each does you a favour or two, once: a job lead, freelance work, a supplier's contact, better delivery terms, an investor meeting or a tip about a building for sale. Rival tycoons: a new option sets when the first one comes forward, at 5 million as before or at 10, 20, 30 or 50 million. A new option, Ride fares: easier passengers (off unless you switch it on), has passengers mind hard stops, bumps and speeding less; everything else about a fare is as before. Car tuning: the custom shop has a Gear change slot (quick shift and sequential gearboxes), a Figures page that reads power, torque, 0 to 100 km/h, top speed, gear change, braking, weight, ride height and grip as standard, now and with the work you choose, and its work now counts when you sell the car: the buyer pays part of what it cost on top of the game's price. Household staff: ring the new Household staff contact to take on a cleaner, a cook, a live-in butler or a mobile mechanic for your home, at the city's going wages. They clean the home, cook dinners into the fridge, and wash, repair and service the cars at home, and while you are there you see them at work. Three new kinds of shop to start in BizMan: an ice cream parlour, a tea bar and a tobacconist. They sell the game's own goods from its own furniture and run as its own shops do, each with its icon, busy hours, help page and ready-made designs. A heavy goods licence for the Freight Truck, the game's largest vehicle: on top of the commercial licence, with its own theory test, a lesson and a driven test in the school's truck, and the courier round offers better paid heavy loads with it.

Fixed
- In a home with an upper floor, a meal is taken to a free seat on the floor you are on, so the seats upstairs no longer hide the table downstairs. With Developer diagnostics on, the log also says why a piece of furniture cannot be picked up to move, or will not go down where you click, and whether the save keeps its new place.
- In a Going Public session, a class or exam now begins at its school desk or university classroom when you get there, as the session hides the game's panel, and a desk with nothing to begin says why in a notice. Work late at a job's desk begins there too.
- A hit-and-run you got away from no longer costs you twice. When a summons follows, the court case takes the traffic camera's bill, so a dismissed case is not followed by a fine, and a conviction's sentence covers it, the damages included.
- A job's working hours now always fall inside its workplace's opening hours, so a shift no longer starts before the place opens (the warehouse job began at 06:00 at places that open at 08:00, and the shut door cost warnings and then the job). A save with such a job has its hours put right and those warnings taken back at load, and a working day whose place is shut at its start, or a rota shift with nobody near open to work for, is excused.

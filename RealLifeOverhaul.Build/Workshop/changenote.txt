0.60.0

New
- Car parks: build one on your own land in build mode with the new Car park button, laid out in bays with a way in from the street, a pay booth and lamps. The plans take a day, City Hall decides in a working day and the works take one to three working days, with crews of yours or a hired builder. The game's own cars park in its bays and your own cars park there free; you let it as public pay parking at a price you set, or by the month to the businesses and rivals nearby, with takings and leases as lines of their own in your books. The city's builders and the rival tycoons put up car parks too, and you can lease bays from them.

Fixed
- In build mode, the Choose a building window no longer draws its last designs over the footer and the toolbar. The list scrolls inside its window, every design card is the same size and the one you pick has a blue edge instead of growing, the window shrinks to fit a larger UI scale, and the heading names the street, for example 2nd Street, instead of showing its id. A new test checks the window.
- You could not drive a car or truck into the warehouses of Uptown and Cedar Heights, because their garage doors had been switched off when the buildings were copied. Each warehouse there now has garage doors that face the street, with the pavement in front kept clear of parked cars, lamp posts, litter and benches, and a car or truck goes in and out through them as at the game's own warehouses. A new test in Options drives your car in and out of the nearest warehouse.

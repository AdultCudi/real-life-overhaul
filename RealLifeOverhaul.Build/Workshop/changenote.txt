New

Fixed
- With more than 2.1 billion in cash, a life event could take 2.1 billion at once, shown as vehicle costs, because the money was too large for the number the mod reads it in. Every charge of the mod now stays within what the thing costs and within your cash, such a charge already in your records is paid back once, and a new test checks the car costs on your cars.
- A save that held one of the mod's moods in the game's own mood list, as an autosave could, loaded with an error, and pressing confirm in the shower, sleep and cooking panels then did nothing. The mod's moods are now in the game's register before the city is built, a mood the game cannot read is taken out of the list on load, and the mod's moods stay out of every save, an autosave too, even when the mod's own start has failed.
- Data engineers and other professionals found through a job ad or a search firm for a data centre never started work, because a data centre has no desks or shifts for the game's employees. A data centre now takes only the crew who look after its racks, on the payroll, and refuses any other job with the reason. Professionals the game had left standing at a data centre are taken off it and wait in the staff list for a desk, and a new test in Options checks your data engineers are where work is done.
- Uptown's plain side walls now have windows, buildings stand where grey boxes lined 1st Avenue's south side, most bare yards behind buildings are built over, and two lots that stood empty are built.

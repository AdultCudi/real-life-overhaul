0.1.4: Buses: nobody waits more than about 20 minutes of the game clock for a bus. A bus now waits at the stop for you instead of leaving after a few seconds, and a notice says when yours has pulled in and how to board. Bus stops are there when you arrive: after loading a save, a taxi ride or the subway, a stop near you is put up at once, and when driving they are put up from further away. A stop where a line ends says that no bus picks up there and where the nearest stop to board at is. The driving school takes its car back: when a lesson or test ends you are asked to step out, and the car can no longer be kept and driven. An officer posted at a junction stands watch and no longer waves at traffic that is following the lights. Boarding is offered beside the bus, not from across the street. Bicycles are faster: about 38 km/h for the city bicycle and 50 for the road bicycle.

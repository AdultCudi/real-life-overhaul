New
- Courses and skills: the city's courses beyond the game's own. Short courses at Manhattan Business School (first aid, barista, bartending, a forklift ticket, a security licence, a driving refresher that takes penalty points off, bookkeeping, customer service) and online ones (a coding bootcamp, digital marketing), and diplomas for electricians, plumbers, carpenters, chefs, hairdressers, beauticians, care workers and IT support. Each has a timetable of classes on set days and hours, homework at home and an exam with a grade, failing and retakes, in the new Campus app and the Planner. They teach skills of your own, 0 to 100, that grow with study and fade a little without use. Professional jobs: work as anything you are qualified for. Twenty-five professions, from developer, data scientist, accountant, lawyer, architect, civil engineer, nurse and teacher to marketing, banking, management up to chief executive, the council, the trades, kitchens, salons, bars, door security and warehouses, each a ladder judged on your own skills and qualifications. The city's businesses, your rivals' among them, its tech companies and the public sector advertise posts, and your rivals' ads on the jobs board are posts too. Apply, attend the interview at the employer or by video, haggle over the offer, then work your days at the office or from home, paid by the minute, in the new Career app with your CV. Working life: reviews twice a year with a rating, rises, bonuses and promotions up to the top seats, a boss with a manner of their own, office matters to answer, overtime and burnout, search firms that come for good people, redundancy in a downturn with severance and unemployment benefit, share options at tech companies, a pension and retirement. Rise to the top of a rival's company for a share of its takings, and declare a business of your own in the same trade or risk being dismissed for it. Working for yourself: freelance contracts from the city's clients, worked at home or at the client's; a practice of your own once qualified, whose name brings more work; side work that an exclusive contract may catch. Leave a tech profession to found a company, bring colleagues to your new firm and pitch with a founder's record the venture firms count, and mind a restraint clause: break it and your old employer takes you to court. University and degrees: Hudson City University in one of the city's large office buildings, with fourteen degrees from computer science, law, nursing (on the hospital wards) and architecture to teaching, then an MBA, an MSc, the Legal Practice Course and a teaching certificate, and twelve professional certificates studied at home (cloud, security, project management, chartered accountant, the bar exam and more). Degrees are classed from a third to a first, fees come in instalments with merit and need grants and the council's subsidy, a student loan on your credit file is repaid from your pay, and graduation day has its ceremony, a Flare post and a story in the City Ledger. Family and the rest: a grown child is offered a place at the university, paid from the college fund and by you, and graduates with a class of their own; tutoring from home in your best subject; a Training Academy of your own as a business type whose staff teach the city's students, and courses at half the fee for your staff while you have one; degrees for your professionals sponsored by the business. The banking licence now asks for a qualified person, and Permadeath's obituary names your best qualification and your career.

New
- Once you are well off, a partner who lives with you may ask to cut back to half days at work. Say yes and they are home more and pay a smaller share of the rent; they can go back to full days from a family conversation. It has its own switch, in a new section of the options page: Your partner and your fortune. With the social life on, the Family contact is gone and family life is face to face: talk to your partner at home, use the new Family button in your fridge's panel for the overview and the rest, and Family matters in your partner's People call for time apart and the in-laws. Standing in another home of yours, text your partner to make it your new home. Your partner now reacts as your cash grows, by how they are: a supportive partner is proud and the home grows warmer, an envious one minds the gap more as you get richer, a little less once you are married. It has its own switch in the same section. Once you are rich, a partner who lives with you may ask either to stop working or to retrain for the job they always wanted. Stopping means no pay from them until they go back to work; retraining takes about three weeks without pay, then they start the new job. While your partner earns nothing, you can ask them to chip in for the rent from their savings. As your cash grows, a partner who lives with you expects a bigger home, evenings out more often, a weekly allowance, a car of their own and in time a second home. Meet it and the home grows warmer; leave it and the home slowly cools. Set the allowance when you talk to your partner at home. It has its own switch in the same section. Meeting your partner's wishes now also brings you closer. The allowance they expect follows how they are with money, whether they earn, marriage and children. A car is really handed over, or bought for them at the game's price, and can be asked back. Your own AI: the people who answer through RLO Texts can answer through an AI service you choose, off unless you choose one. Sign in with ChatGPT to use your own plan, paste your own key for one of eight services, or run a model on this PC. Every reply goes through the same checks as the pack's, and the pack or the written line is used when it fails or is late. The card, with what you typed, goes to the service you chose; your key stays on this PC. Careers from a course or a degree now pay fairly against the gigs: a barista starts on about 70 an hour instead of 18, a junior developer on 120 instead of 38, and the top of a ladder pays three to seven times its first rung. Degrees take three to four weeks instead of eight or more, diplomas two to three, reviews come every two weeks in the game's own year of 60 days (every four at most in a longer year), a good review's bonus is days of pay, and a working day teaches enough to climb the ladder. A walk key: on foot, press F3 to walk at the game's own walking pace, and press it again for your usual pace. Hold the game's Toggle Running key to hurry for a moment. The key can be changed under Controls, and walking has its own switch there.

Fixed
- An outing is no longer arranged at a place that is shut when you meet. The hour and the place are now chosen so that it is open for the whole outing, another place nearby is tried when the nearest is shut, and when everywhere is shut a walk is suggested instead.
- With the social life on, your partner's texts about family life (being stood up, a missed appointment, an arrest) only showed as a notice; they now arrive as texts from them in People.
- With the social life on, your partner's job at home and in family talks is the one People gives them, not a different one; a retired partner stays at home.
- A car your partner has from you is no longer billed for parking while it is theirs, and with their expectations switched off it is yours again, back in the city or left at the nearest repair shop.
- With the social life on, a Family contact you already have stays in your phone with all its messages.
- After your partner retrains for a new job, People shows the new job.
- A good review at Uncle Lou's Street Eats or Five Borough Cabs no longer reads as a wage of 0 an hour. Your own cart and the owner-operator's cab have no basic wage, so the boss now says what you keep, both pay more than the rank below, and a good review there raises your share of what you take.
- The mod's apps on the phone and in the full menu's top row stand in the same order every time, whichever loaded first.
- Notifications in the top right no longer cover the panel of a shift, a gig, a lesson or a driving test: while it shows, they start under it.
- A class and a shift on the same day no longer look like a clash when they fit. A class's panel says how much of it counts, a course's page in Campus says when to leave class for work or where it clashes, and a vacancy's page in Career says the same for your classes.

- Standing: the city reacts to your success. Your wealth, how well you are known and what people think of you as an owner (customers, workers, neighbourhoods, integrity) are on a new page of the Finance app, with what moves each. People in the street, customers in your shops and your staff take notice of you. Reporters stop you in the street, the night show and a magazine invite you, your promises are remembered, and the City Ledger writes about your rise. Your name brings customers or costs them, and walking past somebody who needs help is seen. The city asks you to sponsor events, give to charity, save a shop or answer a complaint; evenings with investors and receptions at City Hall bring new people and partnerships; you can lobby the council; and an official may offer to make an inspection a formality for a bribe, which can come out later.

0.36.0

Fixed
- A roommate no longer goes quiet for the rest of the session when the game has no player character for a moment, as it can while a scene changes.
- The City Ledger delivered to your door no longer gives free mornings after a spell with no home: only the mornings you had paid for wait, as on Newsstand.
- A kiosk no longer charges for today's paper when the paper cannot be opened.
- When you are the one expecting and miss a scan or a class, the clinic now books it again for the next day, as its message says.
- A family errand you have paid for or kept (a scan, a dinner, counselling, a school event, the honeymoon) is no longer lost when another scene is under way: it happens at once instead.
- Load my choices from the file no longer fails when the copy of your current settings cannot be written first; it goes ahead without the copy, as a preset or a reset does.
- Report a bug no longer says it is busy for the rest of the session after a report was cut off part of the way.

Next version (not yet numbered): The Company app has a Rota tab. It shows the game's customer forecast for each hour, the opening hours that pay for their staff, and how many people a business needs. One button fills the week's rota by each employee's skills and demands, and another puts the old rota back. A business or a warehouse can now sell online: the new Web shop app opens an online shop, orders come in from the whole city by the hour, and your staff pick them from the real stock. Orders go out with your own vans or a courier. Late and missing deliveries cost refunds and a star rating, and some orders come back. The day's online takings, refunds and delivery costs are booked each night and count as the business's takings for tax. Cleaners can be shared. Put them on a crew in the new Services app and they go round the businesses on its list, when a floor needs it or at hours you set, on foot or by van. Other businesses in the city offer cleaning contracts with a weekly fee and a standard to keep. A business can be sold. The Services app shows what it is worth line by line. Put it on the market and a rival owner, an investor, your staff or a chain make offers with their own terms, or take a quick sale at a discount. Shops of one type can now trade as a brand, with recognition in each district and standards all its shops share: a known, well-kept brand brings its shops up to 12% more customers. A brand can be franchised: applicants open shops under it, pay an entry fee and a share of their takings every day, and need inspecting, warning and sometimes help. One of your own shops can instead take a franchise of a city chain. Hair keeps its own colour against a lamp. With a car's lights behind or beside your head, dark hair stays dark; before, it could glow as bright as skin and look bald. The Tests section has three entries for hair: one says what your hair is made of, one stands a strong lamp beyond your head for 20 seconds, one switches the hair finish off and on. Traffic now sees people in the road: cars brake for anyone in their way, sound the horn at somebody who steps out close or dawdles, wait, and drive on. People no longer fade out beside a braking car. A driver who cannot stop when an accident is due really hits the person, at the speed that is left, and stops or drives off. Cars that collide on the road slide to rest before the scene is set up, a car that loses a bend ends against what stands there, and a reckless driver who calms down drives on without pulling up. Property is a new app on the phone. Buy run-down buildings at prices that move with the economy, let them to tenants you choose, repair and refurbish them, and sell them for offers; or run an estate agency from an office with your own staff. You can also buy or lease one of the city's hotels and set its rates and staff, and put your own cars out on hire from a lot. The rota planner now shows a ladder of plans for each business: the week as it is, the best week your people can keep, and one plan for each further person worth hiring, with what each adds. Plans count what the game counts: nothing sells at an unstaffed till, a till serves a fixed number an hour, cleanliness, theft, and what an unmet demand costs. You can choose staff first or lean for each business; the choice is saved. Every hour a plan opens or closes is listed with its reason. A plan that needs a hire cannot be written until you have hired. A business cleaned by the cleaning service gets no cleaning shift. Fixed: the game froze for seconds when an ambulance crew stepped out. Fixed: a car in the custom shop shot backwards when the shop closed. The driving test now marks only what you could have helped: a tap of the brake, a light that changes on top of you and where the school left the car are no longer faults. People react to your horn: someone in your way who should not be there hurries clear, and someone with the light in their favour stops and glares. Keep it up near a police officer and you are warned, then fined. Accidents now come from drivers you can watch go wrong, such as speeders, red-light runners, late brakers and drunks at night: they may crash where it happens, run somebody down, drive off and be chased, or be pulled over by a patrol car. The minimap now shows the city's buildings and its water under the roads, in the colours of the game's interface. The city is measured for this once for each version of the game, in the first seconds out in the city. Every table in Finance, Ventures, Company and the Planner now sorts when you click a column heading, can be filtered by kind from a list above it, and ends in a line of totals. Accounts, offers, cash flow, investors, shares and staff show more columns, and the box beside them says more about whatever you pick. A bus stop's panel, and every other panel over a door or desk, now stands beside a building's info box and no longer covers it. Fishing, the dance floor and the chess clock show their timing on one of the game's own bars. While another camera mod drives the camera, this mod's cameras stand aside and come back when it stops. With Voogle Route switched on, this mod's route is drawn on the minimap only, and a new option leaves the route to that mod. You can share your flat. Ring the new Roommate contact to put up a notice or ask a friend: with a second bed, or at least a sofa, somebody moves in, pays a share of the rent every week and is at home when their hours say so. Some are tidy, some are noisy, some are late with the rent, and you can talk it over, wait, or give notice. The rota planner leaves whoever holds a headquarters desk where they are and fills only empty desks. Its advice keeps to hours that need no late opening permit and says what the permit would add. A large business is worked out over a few frames. Fixed: the game froze for seconds when an ambulance crew stepped out. Fixed: a car in the custom shop shot backwards when the shop closed.

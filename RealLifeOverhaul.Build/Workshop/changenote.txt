New
- The most of a company you may hold is an option under the stock market's options: 10% as before, 25%, 50% or all of it. A rival's holding company stops at the quarter its tycoon does not own, and a holding past a lowered figure is kept. Takeaway and plates: food you carry in a bag, and a home dish packed to take from the cooking list, is eaten when you sit down. Click a chair or a bench, rest, and press Eat: the bag becomes the place's tray, a plate on the table or a plate in your lap. Internet speed now matters: a tech firm works faster on a faster office connection and slower on a slow one, more so for game studios and AI labs, and an online shop takes as many orders a day as its building's connection allows, none without one. The Tech app's firm page, the Web shop app and the Utilities plan picker show what each speed means. It has its own switch under the utilities' options.

Fixed
- The first midnight after loading a save no longer stutters from the mod's day. The code every feature runs at midnight was made ready in the very frame the day turned; it is now made ready a little at a time in the first half minute after the load, and nothing the day does changes.

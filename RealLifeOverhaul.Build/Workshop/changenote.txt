Fixed
- A rented scooter is now kept while you are indoors only for the day you rented it. After midnight it is taken back, with a notice, as soon as it is out of sight and nobody is on it, so going indoors every evening no longer keeps it for ever.
- Burgers, hot dogs, pizza, kebabs and other food the game will not let you put in a fridge or a freezer are now kept cold by the fridges and freezers at your shops, up to the room each has, and the Guide and the stock page say how much.

0.2.5

New
- The rota planner counts the goods: a plan sells a product only while there is some, and the page says what runs out, when, and what would keep it on the shelves.
- Bus stops: Choose a stop opens the city map with the stops on it and the game's list of stations beside it, as a subway entrance does.
- On the city map, the mod's buildings light up when their row is ticked, as the game's own do: police stations, the courthouse, hotels, driving schools and more.
- One calendar: a month is a twelfth of the year you chose, and the seasons are the weather's everywhere. Car insurance runs a month; childcare and school fees follow that month at the same cost by the day.
- Wages follow prices once: rising prices no longer make staff ask for raises on top of the city's cost of living. The city's wage floor counts raises, and the city government's levies, grants and fees move with prices.
- The Company features' wages, marketing and licences show in the game's own daily statement, and the game's bank sizes loans by that profit.

Fixed
- The search box in Contacts was out of sight to the left of the app; it now has its own line above the list.
- The minimap turned with the way you walked; it now turns with the camera.
- A car towed after a crash could stay behind as a shell you walked through; the whole car now goes to the space.
- The game could freeze for two seconds as an ambulance crew got out with the stretcher; that is now a moment's work.
- A subway entrance opened the mod's list of stations instead of the game's subway map; the game's map is back, and the journey still takes its time.

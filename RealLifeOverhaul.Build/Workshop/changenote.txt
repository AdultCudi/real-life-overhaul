1.0.13

Fixed
- The Mods page in Options gave way to the game's own screen when another mod had a drop-down option. Drop-down options from other mods now show on the page.
- Updating the mod no longer costs a tech firm its products. A firm whose business cannot be read for a moment is left alone, a copy of your tech state is kept in the save, and a save that was already hit is put right once.
- The desk session card no longer stays up empty after Work here, a cancel or the walk to the desk, Esc always closes it and gives the keys back, and the status bar's clock now keeps the same time as the phone's.
- Delivery trips now share a full vehicle between all the stops of a route instead of filling the first stops only, say on the route's page where the goods are, and tell you once a day when and why goods did not reach a shop. Food thrown out for age no longer shows as sent from a warehouse.
- Campus now says where each exam is sat and by which day, with a Show on map button, the school's study desks are marked while an exam of yours is open, and a refused course, job or promotion names what is short, what it needs and what you have.
- The police no longer fine or warn you for walking in the road while you are helping someone, at an accident, a scene, a street event on a closed road, a job's marker or your own car at the kerb, nor in the moments after you get up from being knocked down. Walking in the road elsewhere is still jaywalking. Helping a friend move now tells you to pick up a box beside them first, and the marker points at the boxes until you carry one.

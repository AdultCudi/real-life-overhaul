0.40.4

New
- The People contact shows each person's age, which goes up by a year on their birthday.

Fixed
- A friend who suggests an outing on a call no longer turns it down when you say yes. Somebody with no free hour in the next two days, or on a rough day, says so as soon as you ask about plans.
- The mod's moods, such as A nice moment and Did the right thing, no longer grow to hundreds of days each time a save is loaded, as happened in Going Public. Each mood now has a limit, and a save where one grew has it cut back once when it loads.
- A tech firm moved to another building by a moving company lost its products, sales, contracts, staff and investors. They now move with it, and a save where they were left at the old building gets them back.
- Your desk at work can always be walked to. A desk the office layout had boxed in, for example at an angle against the end of a long table, could not be reached when clicked; now another place by its chair or another desk is taken.

0.1.5: Minimap and GPS: a small map of the streets around you is on screen whenever you are out in the city, with a route along the roads to your destination, the next turn, the distance left and the time you should arrive. The marker goes when you arrive. Two quick clicks on the city map set a destination. Buses and the subway now use the game's own interface: point at a bus stop, its shelter or the standing bus and click. The stop shows the line and the next bus, and "Choose a stop" opens a list of every bus stop in the city with the time and the fare. Pick a stop while you wait and you board by yourself when the bus pulls in. A line's last stop can be boarded from. Buses: nobody waits more than about 20 minutes of the game clock for a bus, and a bus waits at the stop for you instead of leaving after a few seconds. Bus stops are there when you arrive: after loading a save, a taxi ride or the subway, a stop near you is put up at once, and when driving they are put up from further away. The driving school takes its car back: when a lesson or test ends you are asked to step out, and the car can no longer be kept and driven. An officer posted at a junction stands watch and no longer waves at traffic that is following the lights. Bicycles are faster: about 38 km/h for the city bicycle and 50 for the road bicycle.

New
- More people to meet: the gym, your job, the coffee cart, your street, your shop, Flare and evenings out keep bringing new faces, one a day at most, up to 400 people in your life. With that many, only those closest to you, family, your partner and anyone you met lately get in touch on their own. Call the others whenever you like. Interviews at a place: at an employer's you walk in at the door, the screen goes black while the interview lasts and you come back out at the same door. A video interview from home sits you down at your computer, if your home has one. Managers have a directory of their own on the Company app's Managers tab: regional managers by district, with the places they look over and who they stand in for, the managers of businesses, of factories and offices and of warehouses in lists of their own, the places with no manager, and a Needs you list of what to look at. Each list sorts by any column and filters by district, a manager's panel shows their pay and leads to their regional manager, and the first page counts every group, what it costs a month and who is off sick. Meals and music: when you eat and a free seat is near, you sit down with your food in front of you, as customers do in a fast food place. A dish cooked at home is carried to the table and eaten there, and a speaker playing music at home offers Dance in its panel. Dancing to the music lifts your mood and costs no energy.

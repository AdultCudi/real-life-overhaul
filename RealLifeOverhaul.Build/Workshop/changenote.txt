New
- Brazilian Portuguese: the mod follows the language the game is set to. A first translation; tell us what reads wrong.
- The rota gives every employee the weekly hours they ask for, never fewer and never more, and puts shifts on the hours customers come.
- The more skilled get their hours first. Staff the business has no hours for are named, so you can let them go.
- Company app: raise an underpaid employee to the going rate at any time, without waiting for them to ask.
- Company app: the buttons that answer a raise, a rival's offer, a strike or overtime are at the top of the page.
- A test tool under Troubleshooting says what is blocking you where you stand.
- Bank apps: each of the city's banks has its own app with your account, loans, card, offers and rating.
- Contacts: real faces for people and logos for services, all from the game, and a search box.
- Police stations are furnished with the game's own desks, computers, lockers, plants and lamps.

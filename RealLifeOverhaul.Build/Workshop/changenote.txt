0.8.2

New
- Interiors and decor: each part now has a strength in the options instead of a switch. District standards Off, Gentle, Standard or Strict; the reward over the standard and the prices Off, Low, Conservative, Standard or Generous; the customers decor brings Off or 5% to 20%; wear Off, Slow, Standard or Fast. The defaults give the figures of 0.8.0, a part you had switched off stays off, and the presets set them. The interior's customers are now a part of their own beside the rest of the mod's, so the strongest decor choice is not lost under them. A business that sells nothing just now, closed, with nothing on its shelves, nobody on shift or its last customers all turning its prices down, gets nobody extra from its interior until it sells again, and the Interior tab says why. An office that takes clients, such as a law firm or an agency, is booked more of them an hour ahead for its interior and decor, as Meaningful Ambience does: 5% more at its best by default, up to 20%, never more than its staffed workstations left free. The Interior tab's right column has four pages now, turned with arrows: what each figure does, every placed item that counts with its points, today's customers by where they came from as the game counts them (the street, your marketing, interiors and decor, the rest of the mod, Meaningful Ambience), and the game's own rules for the business, what its customers want and what it needs to open, each met or missing with the items that meet it. Both columns scroll.

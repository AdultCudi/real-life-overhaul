0.7.1

New
- Bus rides now use the game's own activity panel once you are seated and the doors close. Let it run and the bus drives its route through the city in real time, calling at every stop on the way, where people get on and off, until it reaches yours. Use the panel's fast forward to ride at the game's fast speed, or skip to pass the rest of the ride under a fade as before. Move, or press the panel's button, to get off at the next stop; nothing is given back. A save during the ride puts you at the last stop the bus called at. A ride with a change of bus still passes under a fade.

Fixed
- A pole, a bin or a bench by a bus stop could stop you reaching the bus's door. Street furniture on the kerb by the doors and on the way to them from the stop is now cleared, you are walked to a place by the door that can be reached, and as a last resort you get on by the middle door and walk up to the validator to pay.

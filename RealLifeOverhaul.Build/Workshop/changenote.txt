0.1.4: Bus stops are there when you arrive. After loading a save, a taxi ride or the subway, a stop near you is put up at once, and when driving they are put up from further away. Before, a stop was left out for as long as you were looking at its place. A stop where a line ends now says that no bus picks up there and where the nearest stop to board at is. The driving school takes its car back: when a lesson or test ends you are asked to step out, and the car can no longer be kept and driven.

1.0.5

Fixed
- On a game build older than the one the mod is made for, the mod's notice about it listed every part that was switched off. The list was taller than the screen, its button was out of reach and the main menu could not be used. The notice is short now: it says which build the mod needs and how to update the game in Steam, names a few parts and counts the rest, closes with its button, Esc or Enter, and shows once each time the game starts. Player.log has the whole list. No text of the mod can outgrow the game's question box any more.
- With no business of your own, Flare kept warning that a rival was talking down a business of yours. A rival now runs down a business only where there is one, its neighbours' as well as yours, and the notice names your business and the rival, comes once, and opens the messages you answer in.
- Flare warned that reviews of a business of yours had turned when the business was somebody else's, and said so again every day. It now speaks only of a business of your own, once.
- Mom and your friends asked again and again for a tour of a business you did not have. They ask only when you have one, once, and ringing them or giving the tour settles it. Nobody texts you the same line twice running, and a friend does not write the same comment twice running under your Flare posts.
- A street chess table, a cart or other street life could be set up on a crossing in the road. They now stand only on pavement, never on a crossing, a road, a bus zone or parking, and one whose place stops being pavement is taken in.
- A few other lines spoke of your businesses when you had none: a stranger's greeting, a grown child's message, the season's news and a friend's call.
- After a part of the RLO interface had been switched off for repeated errors, Report a bug opened a card that could not be seen or answered, and it took every click until the game was closed. Report a bug now opens the game's own text box whenever the interface cannot draw its card, and a part that is switched off after errors lets go of everything it held over the screen.
- An error in one screen of the RLO interface could switch the whole interface off. Now only that screen goes back to the game's own, and the notice and Player.log name what failed.
- The notice about a part switched off after repeated errors said it would be back when you loaded a save. It stays off until the game is started again, and the notice now says so.
- With the RLO interface on, clicking some items, such as ones another mod adds, made the interface fail again and again until it switched itself off. The item's card now opens as it should.

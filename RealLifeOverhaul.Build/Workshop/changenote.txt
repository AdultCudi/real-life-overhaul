1.0.25

Fixed
- The goods list of a stock buying plan on the RLO interface shows the weekly limit, index, last week's order and last week's sales in columns of their own, so none is cut off.
- The Property rent roll on the RLO interface no longer draws its empty message over the lists under it.
- Buying a business from a rival no longer stops Services, Sell for the session, and a purchase that stopped half way in an earlier version is finished.
- A construction job above your builder licence now says how many floors it has and what the licence for them asks, on the job and when you bid, and such a bid is refused instead of won and stuck.
- A job's time limit now allows for the materials to be delivered stage by stage.
- The limit of 64 managers in the Company app is now 256. Own work contracts show their hours and what they pay an hour, in both interfaces.
- A freelance contract you took on no longer disappears after you save and load, because finished contracts could crowd the newest out of the save.
- A desk session you are sat at when you save is carried on after you load, from the minutes already sat.
- The notice of rivals' offers matched by themselves is no longer lost when the game is saved just after midnight.
- Discarding a candidate in the new interface takes one press, as it does in the game.
- A help-wanted board whose building could not take it when the city loaded is asked again when you are near, instead of being left out for the whole session.
- A cleaning or stocking gig at a cinema or theatre lets you through the ticket gates, so every screen can be reached.
- The notice that a vehicle of yours was moved out of a bus stop's bay now comes every time and names the vehicle.
- Patrol cars are no longer parked beside a marked crossing, where they stood in the middle of the road.

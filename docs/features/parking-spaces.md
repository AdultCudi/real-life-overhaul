# Parking spaces

## In a sentence

A player buys or rents a single parking space in the car park under a building, without buying or renting the building: a car left there is parked and charged by the game's own rules, and the charge for it is paid back each night; the rival tycoons hold spaces by the same rules and the city's people hold the rest.

A player asked for it: "buy one parking space in a building".

## What the game does

- A building may have a car park under it (`CityBuildingController.undergroundParkingEntrance`, an `UndergroundParkingEntrance` with a `parkingNumber`): the game has five, numbered 1 to 5, under residential and office buildings, and the mod's own copies of such buildings carry one with a number of their own ([New buildings](new-buildings.md)).
- A car driven in is kept there by the game (`VehicleInstance.streetName` is `ba:street_parking`, `streetNumber` the car park's number, `parkingState` Legal). No space comes with a building: every car in every car park, the owner's too, is charged `ParkingSimulator.UndergroundParkingHourlyFee`, 25 an hour (`ParkingSimulator.RunHourly`), taken the next morning as "public parking" (`RunDaily`). A car being driven and a thing carried by hand are not charged.
- The game's hour is `GlobalEvents.onNewHour`, raised just before `ParkingSimulator.RunHourly` in the same frame.

## What the feature does

- **A car park's spaces** (`SpaceRules.Capacity`): what the building code asks of the building over it, by its use and floor area (`PlansRules.ParkingNeeded`, `BuildCatalogue.ParkingPerSpace`: a space for every 120, 60, 80 or 200 m2 of homes, shops, offices or storage), at least 8 and at most 60. Spaces are numbered from 1.
- **Who holds them.** The player and the rival tycoons by records in the save; the city's people by a rule with nothing saved (`SpaceRules.CityHolds`): each space is drawn afresh every 45 days, on a day of its own, with the chance of the district's share (`CityShare`: 500 thousandths and 20 more for each dollar an hour the street's parking costs there, `NeighborhoodData.parkingPrice`, within 550 and 900). A space nobody holds is free, and the lowest numbered free space is the one offered.
- **The rent** (`RentMonth`): a term is 30 days, and its rent is 17% of the hourly charge round the clock for the term (the term and share the mod's surface car parks lease a bay by, `CarParkRules.LeaseDays` and `LeaseShare`), by what the district does to a property's value (`EstateRules.DistrictValue`, 840 to 1160 thousandths), named in tens: 3,060 in a middling district.
- **The price** (`Price`): the rent over a year of the save's length at the property market's yield for storage (84 thousandths, `EstateCatalogue.YieldMiddle`), by the property market's level (`EstateState.Real`; 1000 where the property business does not run), named in hundreds: 72,900 in a year of 60 days at the market's long-run level, 145,700 in one of 120.
- **Buying** (`SpaceDirector.Buy`): the city's car parks are read again, then it is refused before any money (`SpaceRules.MayTake`) with the option off, in a Going Public session, for a car park that is not there or under a building of the player's own, at 12 spaces in all or 4 in one car park, with no space free, or without the cash. The question box states the price and the space it asked about; if the price or the space is no longer what the city now has (`SpaceRules.Moved`), nothing is taken, the player is told so and the page is asked again, so the price charged is always the price shown. Then the price is taken through `BizBooks.Book` and the space is the player's. **Sold by** on the page names the rival the game gives as the building's owner (`BuildingRegistration.buildingOwnerRivalId`), otherwise "The building's landlord", the general name for whoever lets the city's buildings. It is a label only: the game keeps no purse for a building's owner or a landlord, so the price goes to nobody. It leaves the player's books and cash as the price of a building bought from the city does, and a rival who owns the building is not paid either.
- **Renting** (`Rent`): the same refusals, the first term paid at once. On the day after a term the next is taken; without the cash, or once the player has given it up (`SetEnding`), the space goes back. Nothing of a term is paid back, and time the option was off or the save was not played is never asked for (`RentPaid`).
- **Selling back** (`Sell`): the owner pays the lower of what was paid and today's price, less the property market's agent's fee of 15 thousandths (`SpaceRules.Back`); what the question box said is what is paid, or nothing is sold. Never as much as was paid, so no buying and selling makes money, whatever the market does between. A rented space is never sold.
- **How a space ends: one rule** (`SpaceRules.Close`, the same for every way). Sold back, or settled because the building has become the player's (see below): the lower of what was paid and the price, less the fee. A bought space whose car park somebody else took away: what was paid, in full and never more, because it was not the player's doing; if the building was the player's when last seen (`OwnSeen`) it is their doing and the lower of what was paid and the last price seen (`PriceSeen`), less the fee, applies. A rented space: the days of its term not used since its car park went, never more than a term, booked where the rent was booked; a rented space in a building that has become the player's runs to the end of its term and then ends, with nothing paid back.
- **The car parks there by the game's own rules.** Nothing of the game's parking is changed. Each game hour (the game's own event, raised just before the game charges its cars) the director counts the player's cars the game is about to charge in each car park, by the game's own test (`SpaceGame.CarsIn`), as far as the player's spaces there go (`Entitled`, `Covered`), each for its vehicle and the day it was counted (`SpaceDay.Hour`). A rented space covers the days its rent is paid for and not the first hour of the day after, even before the day's pass has ended or renewed it. The game takes a day's charges on the morning after it (`ParkingSimulator.RunDaily`, `VehicleInstance.unpaidParkingAmount`), and the day's pass then pays back 25 an hour, as a line of its own (`reallife_transaction_space_refund`), for the hours of the days before today and only for vehicles still in the save: a car sold or scrapped before the morning was never charged, so nothing is paid back for it (`SpaceRules.Refund`). The first hour of the new day (counted as the day turns, charged the morning after) waits for the next pass. An hour is counted once, however often the event is raised, across a save and a load, and after a long skip or time with the option off only the hours the director saw are counted; a vehicle is paid back for a day of hours at most. This was chosen over lowering the vehicle's own unpaid amount because the game adds its hour just after the event and takes the whole in `RunDaily`, so counting by vehicle and paying only for vehicles the game charged is the exact one that touches nothing of the game's.
- **A building of the player's own.** Its free spaces are the player's already: nothing is bought or sold there, and the charge is paid back for as many cars as there are spaces nobody else holds. Bought spaces the player held there before they bought the building are settled at once, within the second the building becomes theirs (`SpaceDay.Owned`, `SpaceGame.OwnersChanged`), as if sold back (the lower of what was paid and the price, less the fee), with a notice (`reallife_space_note_own`): nobody is left to sell to, and a space kept would come back at its full price if the building were demolished (only the player's own Uptown buildings can be, `ConstructionDirector.RedevelopRefusal`). A space rented there ends at its term with nothing more to pay.
- **A car park that goes, or shrinks** (a building demolished, rebuilt as another, or rebuilt smaller: `Looked`, `Seen`, `Gone`, `Close`). A space whose car park is not found, or has fewer spaces than the space's number, is marked on the day's pass, asked no rent, and after 3 days missing is settled by the rule above. One that stands again before then (rebuilt with its number, or stood late at a load) keeps its spaces. A building that is sold keeps its car park and its spaces; only the seller of the next space changes.
- **The rival tycoons** (`SpaceRules.RivalLook`, the day's pass, never in a session). Every 20 days, each on a day of their own, a tycoon in play without a space buys, one time in two, the lowest free space of a car park drawn for them at the player's price, only while their worth stays over their floor (the worth the rivals raise them to each day, `RivalRules.At`). A tycoon's worth is what they own, so a space bought is worth what was paid and their worth does not move: the daily growth towards their aim (`RivalRules.Grow`) has nothing to refill, and the space is not free. One who has held a space 90 days sells it back, one time in four, for what the owner pays the player, and their worth falls by what the sale loses (the fee). One space each. Never in a building of the player's: its spaces are not sold without the player, and if the player buys the building theirs are settled as the player's are. Their space is a line on the rich list and on their panel (`RivalBridges.SpaceLine`).
- **Where the player finds it.** The Property app, where buildings are bought: each building with a car park is a row of the **Market** tab (the price, and "3 free", "You hold 2", "Full" or "Your building"); its page gives the price, the rent, the seller, who holds the spaces, **Buy a space** and **Rent a space** (each asked first in the game's question box) and the player's spaces there with **Sell it back** or **End the rental**. Every space held is a row of the **Mine** tab, where the player's property shows. The same in the game's frame and on the kit. The city's car parks are read again as each page is built (`SpaceDirector.Open`, once in a frame at most), so a page never shows the last pass's figures. A space's row is in no total of the tables (`TableRow.NoTotal`): the Market's total of prices and the Mine tab's worth, rent and year figures are the buildings' alone. A car park's district is listed in the districts filter beside the buildings', and the kind filter has a kind for it.

## Renting beside buying

Renting by the term belongs beside buying: the mod's surface car parks already let a bay by a term of 30 days ([Construction](construction.md), "Parking lots"), so a space under a building is rented by the same term and share, and its price is that rent at the property market's yield.

## The option

**Parking spaces to buy or rent** (`parking_spaces`, on), under Property. Off: nothing is counted, paid back, taken or sold, the tycoons do not look, and every car in a car park pays by the hour as before; the spaces stay in the save and count again when it is on.

## Going Public

A space is the player's own record, and what is paid back is the player's own charge. A car park is shared, and nothing is sent, so in a session no space is bought, rented or sold (refused before any money) and the tycoons rest. The day's pass keeps the player's own spaces running on the player's own money, and nothing else (`SpaceDay.Pass` with the session): their cars' charges are still paid back and a rented space's next term is taken or the space ends. No car park is settled as gone, smaller or the player's now (the city's buildings are the host's, and this machine cannot say a car park is gone), and nothing of a tycoon's or of another player's changes: no tycoon's worth moves and none is told anything. They are settled on the first pass out of a session. Who of the city's people holds a space comes from the save's seed and the day.

## The save

One line, `reallife:parking_spaces`, version 1: the header (the seed, the last day and hour, the sums paid back and paid), the spaces held (the car park, the space, who, what was paid or the rent, the day, the day paid to, given up, missing since, the price and the owner last seen) and the car-hours counted and not yet paid back (the day, the car park, the vehicle, the hours: part `p`). A save from before has no line. A line from a newer version is read as far as it goes; numbers are written in the invariant culture. In the day's result a space bought, sold back or settled is capital; its rent, a rent paid back for the days a car park was gone (booked where the rent was) and the charges paid back count.

## Cost

Nothing per frame and no Update. A few comparisons a second, and once a second a look at the owner of the building of each bought space held (at most a dozen, no walk of the city); once a game hour a walk of the player's vehicles for each car park where the player has a space; once a day, at a load, when a page is built, before a purchase and when the hour finds a car park missing a walk of the city's buildings (no search of the scene).

## Tests

- `EconomyCheck --bizasks` (`BizAsksChecks.cs`, `BizAsksChecks.Day.cs`): the numbers against their sources; a car park's spaces, the city's share and who holds a space; the rent and the price; no profit from buying and selling over two hundred swings of the market; every refusal before money; a price or space that moved since the question box; no space twice; the limits and a full car park; the rent's terms over more than a year, after time away and across a save in the middle of a term; the hour driven through the very rule the director calls (counted once, a save and a load in the middle, a time skip, the option off and on, a car park found late); the charge paid back only for vehicles the game charged, across the midnight; the first hour after a rental; how a space ends (a market that fell and a building bought, a car park taken away, one that shrank, a rent paid back where it was booked); a shared city; the tycoons over 900 days; the save (old, newer, damaged, a decimal comma); the page and each button; the sources (money only through the one call, the refusal before the money, a session, the option off, the game's own hour); the texts in eleven languages. Each rule was broken once to see its check fail.

## Not seen in the game

Everything above is built and checked offline. Not seen in play: the rows on the Market and Mine tabs, the car park's page and the question boxes, the charge coming back as its line the morning after a car stood in a space (and not for a car sold that day), the number of spaces each of the game's five car parks comes to, a bought space being settled the moment the player buys its building, and a tycoon's line on the rich list.

## Two made buildings with one car park number

`GateRules.ParkNumber` draws a made building's number from its id in a range of a billion above the game's own; `SpaceGame.Parks` keeps one car park for a number. The Gates check holds that none of the plan's 500 and more lots shares a number (`GateParks` in `GateChecks.cs`), so no building's car park is hidden today; a collision among new ids is possible only in the odds of a few in a hundred thousand for the whole city, and would also join their cars in the game's own lookup, which is the made buildings' to keep unique, not the spaces'.

## Left out

- A building of the player's own sells no space to a tycoon or to the city's people: the mod has no call by which a rival buys part of a player's building without the player (`SpaceRules.RivalLook` passes such a car park by).
- The seller's money: the game keeps no purse for a building's owner (`BuildingRegistration.buildingOwnerRivalId` is a name), so the price is not added to anybody's worth.

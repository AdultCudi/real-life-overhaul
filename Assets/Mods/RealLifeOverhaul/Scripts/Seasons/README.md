# `Scripts/Seasons`

The seasons: what the player's customers buy follows the season of the calendar, and on some winter days it snows, with flakes falling, the ground white, and traffic and people in the street slower.

## Read first

- `SeasonRules.cs`: the goods' table, the season of a day, the snow of a day and how much lies (game-free).
- `SeasonDirector.cs`: once a second, the goods, the snow and the news.
- `SeasonGame.cs`: the only file that touches the game.

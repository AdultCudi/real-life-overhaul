# `Scripts/Designs`

Ready-made designs: the game's interior installation firms offer the mod's own business types (the Car Showroom, the five tech firms, the Training Academy) designs as they do the game's, made from the game's own designs and furniture, at the game's own prices.

## Read first

- `DesignCatalogue.cs`: which of the game's designs each type's are made from, and what a Car Showroom keeps (game-free).
- `DesignRules.cs` and `DesignPlan.cs`: the folders the firms read, the names, the showroom's cut round its lot, the list of the mod's files (game-free).
- `DesignsDirector.cs`: when the designs are put in place and taken away.
- `DesignsGame.cs`: the only file that touches the game.

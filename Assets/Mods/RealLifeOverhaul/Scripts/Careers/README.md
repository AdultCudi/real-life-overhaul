# `Scripts/Careers`

Education and careers: the city's courses and the player's own skills, the professions at the city's employers, working life, working for oneself, the university, the family's schooling and an academy of the player's own.

## Read first

- `CareersDirector.cs`: the one component, its parts in partial files.
- `Study/StudyCatalogue.cs`: every course, exam, fee and grant (game-free).
- `Work/ProCatalogue.cs`: the twenty-five professions (game-free).
- `CareersBridges.cs`: what it asks of the others.

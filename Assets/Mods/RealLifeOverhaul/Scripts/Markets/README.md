# `Scripts/Markets`

The city's stock market: the market on the game's clock, the Stocks app, bonds, the player's own company listed, orders that wait, trading bots, and the raw materials exchange with its futures and options (Derivatives).

## Read first

- `MarketDirector.cs`: the market on the game's clock.
- `MarketEngine.cs`: the market itself (game-free).
- `MarketApp.cs`: the Stocks app.

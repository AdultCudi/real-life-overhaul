# `Scripts/Economy/Dealer`

The Car Showroom: a business type of the mod's own that the player founds in a retail building and runs with the game's own staff, hours and money. Its stock is real cars on the lot inside, bought new, from private owners and at auction, and sold with finance and warranties; its floor (Floor) has visitors, bays and photos.

## Read first

- `DealerDirector.cs`: the business, kept going.
- `DealerCatalogue.cs`: what it makes and what bounds it.
- `DealerApp.cs`: the Dealership app.

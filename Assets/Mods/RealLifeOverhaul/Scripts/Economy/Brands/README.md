# `Scripts/Economy/Brands`

Brands and franchises: a brand made from a shop, its recognition and standards, what it adds to its member shops' footfall, its campaigns, shared prices and hours; franchising it to applicants who pay a royalty; and one of the player's own shops under a city chain.

## Read first

- `BrandsDirector.cs`: keeps it going.
- `BrandRules.cs`: the rules and the balance (game-free).
- `BrandsGame.cs`: what is read from and done to the game.

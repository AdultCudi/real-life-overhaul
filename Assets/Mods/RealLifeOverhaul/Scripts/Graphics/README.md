# `Scripts/Graphics`

Realistic graphics: a look for the whole picture, chosen on the options page, that follows the clock, the weather and the season by setting sliders of work the game's renderer already does in every frame.

## Read first

- `GraphicsRules.cs`: every figure of every look, the table of every setting a look may write, the budgets (game-free).
- `GraphicsDirector.cs`: when a look is worked out and written. It has no Update.
- `GraphicsGame.cs`: the only file that touches the engine and the game.

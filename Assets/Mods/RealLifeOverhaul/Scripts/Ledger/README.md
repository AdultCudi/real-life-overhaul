# `Scripts/Ledger`

The City Ledger as a paper the player has in their hands: today's edition, a subscription delivered to the door, two newspaper kiosks near the home, and the copy by the door at home.

## Read first

- `LedgerDirector.cs`: the edition, the round, the kiosks.
- `LedgerRules.cs`: the rules (game-free).
- `LedgerPage.cs`: the page, in the game's full menu.
- `LedgerBridges.cs`: what it asks of the others.

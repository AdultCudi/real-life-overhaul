# `Scripts/Hustles`

Legal hustles: the Marketplace app, where the city's people sell used goods and cars that the player buys, collects or has brought home, fixes, cleans and sells on; and freelance visits clients ask for, done at their door or left to a helper.

## Read first

- `HustleDirector.cs`: the state, the day and the hour.
- `HustleCatalogue.cs`: every figure (game-free).
- `HustleRules.cs`: listings, worth, haggling, repairs, buyers and ratings (game-free).
- `HustleGame.cs`: the only file that touches the game.

# `Scripts/Street`

Talking to anybody in the street: walk up to one of the game's walkers and Talk, in the game's own panel and dialogue box. The game decides everything; the words are written lines, or RLO Texts' when it has them in time.

## Read first

- `StreetDirector.cs`: the offer; its parts `.Talk` and `.Outcomes` the conversation; `StreetHold.cs` the person held.
- `StreetRules.cs` and `StreetRules.Choices.cs`: what comes of it (game-free).
- `StreetStore.cs`: the save.

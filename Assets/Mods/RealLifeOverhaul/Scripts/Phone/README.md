# `Scripts/Phone`

Callable phone contacts and their conversations, and the contacts' pictures (Contacts).

## Read first

- `PhoneContacts.cs`: adds the callable contacts.
- `Contacts/ContactCatalogue.cs`: every contact of the mod's.
- `Contacts/ContactPictures.cs`: their pictures, from the game's own assets.
- `Contacts/ContactGroups.cs`: the groups of the Contacts app (each contact's group is its catalogue entry's `Group`), then `ContactGroupsGame.cs` and `ContactGroupsRow.cs` for the game's side.

# `Scripts/People`

The mod's street characters and the scenes they play: the pool of twelve every scene shares, uniforms and kit, poses, the ambulance crew's way and its stretcher.

## Read first

- `ActorPool.cs`: the twelve characters every scene shares.
- `ActorRole.cs`: one person's part in a scene.
- `StreetActor.cs`: one character: walk, stand, pose.
- `CrewWay.cs`: the ambulance crew's one way out and back.

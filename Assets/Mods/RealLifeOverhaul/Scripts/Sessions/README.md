# `Scripts/Sessions`

Desk sessions: every hands-on management session of the living economy is held at the owner's own desk in a firm's office, on the game's own activity panel, or by a manager the player hired. The kinds come from the features; the first is the tech firms' weekly team meeting (Wiring/Wiring.SessionLinks.cs, Tech/People/MeetingRules.cs).

## Read first

- `SessionRules.cs`: a kind of session, the one registry, and the rules (game-free).
- `SessionDirector.cs`: the director, and its parts in partial files.
- `SessionGame.cs`: everything it reads from or does to the game.

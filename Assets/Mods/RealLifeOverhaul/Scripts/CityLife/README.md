# `Scripts/CityLife`

The living city: people at work and about their day on the pavements near the player, at most three sites and eight people.

## Read first

- `CityLifeDirector.cs`: what is out and where, and the test buttons.
- `CityCatalogue.cs`: every activity, one entry each.
- `CityActivity.cs`: the base every activity shares.
- `Activities/ChatActivity.cs`: a short activity of two people to copy.

# `Scripts/Civic`

A city government with a mayor, a council, elections and policies, the player standing for office, and construction sites that progress over days.

## Read first

- `CivicDirector.cs`: the clock and the save.
- `CivicCatalogue.cs`: the levers and every number a policy changes (game-free).
- `CivicGovernment.cs`: mayor, council, elections, polls, petitions.
- `CivicBridges.cs`: what it asks of the others.

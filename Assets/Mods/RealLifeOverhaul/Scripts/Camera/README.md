# `Scripts/Camera`

The third-person camera on foot (driving the game's own pedestrian camera), the chase camera when driving, the crosshair, and standing aside when another author's mod drives the camera.

## Read first

- `ThirdPersonCamera.cs`: the camera on foot and mouse look.
- `ChaseCamera.cs`: the camera behind the car.
- `CameraYield.cs`: standing aside for the game's own camera moments and for other mods.
- `BridgeHold.cs`: the bridge the player is on kept in sight under the close cameras.

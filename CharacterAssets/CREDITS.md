## What the game contributes, and what it does not

- The generators read the game's head and hair meshes and its skeleton from the local installation
  (`scripts/extract_reference.py`, into the git-ignored `_reference/`) and build *around* them: where the
  skull is, and over which part of the scalp each of the game's hairstyles lies. That footprint, a
  256 px mask, is a measurement of the game's mesh; none of its vertices, triangles, UVs or textures are
  copied into the output.
- At runtime the mod clones a material the game is already drawing with and puts the new texture in it,
  and it copies the bind poses from the game's head mesh in memory. Nothing of that is in the bundle.
- At runtime the hair finish makes, in memory only, a copy of each strand texture the player's hair uses with its
  alpha raised. That copy is never written anywhere.
- `calibration/` and `evidence/` hold renders and screenshots of the game's character (the player's own shots and
  the preview's), kept to show what was compared; no mesh or texture of the game's is in the repository.
- Preview renders (`previews/`, git-ignored) show the game's head and hair; they are for checking the work
  locally and are not distributed.

## Looked at and not used

- MakeHuman "system assets" hair (afro01, bob01, bob02, braid01, long01, ponytail01, short01 to short04; CC0,
  https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html): solid sculpted shells
  of 1 700 to 5 200 vertices with a painted texture. They read as a wig, which is the complaint this work
  answers, and are well below the game's own strand hair close up. Rejected on quality, not on licence.
- The mod's own generated strand-card hair and beards (`scripts/hair_styles.py`, `scripts/build_beards.py`,
  kept as source): in the game each card lit as a separate pale lump. Not built into the bundle.

## Shoes, second round (laced boot, loafer, ballet flat, court shoe)

| Asset | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `feet_<m/f>_boot`, `feet_<m/f>_loafer`, `feet_f_flat`, `feet_f_heel` (6 shoe meshes), `feet_<boot/loafer/flat/heel>_n` (4 normal maps), `feet_<style>_<colour>` (16 colour maps) | modelled and painted by `scripts/build_shoes.py` (`STYLES2`); no external mesh or texture | made for this mod; CC0 1.0 |

The game's own shoes and feet are measured for these and nothing else: where the foot's skin is (ray-cast sections of
`FemNakedFeet` for the flat), where the skin part of the game's pumps ends (`FemSuitShoes`: the edge the court shoe's
top line runs along, a little way in), and the skin weights and Fat/Slim/Musc deltas at the closest point of the
game's nearest shoe. No vertex, triangle, UV or texture of the game's is in the output.
